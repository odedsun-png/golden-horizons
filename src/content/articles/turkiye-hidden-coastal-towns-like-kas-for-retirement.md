# Türkiye — Hidden Coastal Towns Like Kaş for Retirement

Nestled along the turquoise waters of the Mediterranean, Türkiye’s hidden coastal towns like Kaş offer a unique blend of history, culture, and natural beauty, making them ideal retirement destinations for those seeking a serene yet vibrant lifestyle. With its charming cobblestone streets, ancient ruins, and welcoming community, Kaş invites retirees to experience a slower pace of life amid stunning landscapes and rich traditions. Here, you can spend your days exploring hidden coves, savoring local cuisine, and immersing yourself in the warm embrace of Turkish hospitality.

## Why Retire Here

Kaş is a picturesque town that captures the essence of Mediterranean living. Located on the Lycian Coast, it boasts breathtaking views of the azure sea and rugged mountains. The town itself is a delightful mix of narrow streets lined with whitewashed houses adorned with bougainvillea. Daily life in Kaş revolves around its vibrant harbor, where fishing boats and yachts bob in the gentle waves. Retirees can enjoy leisurely strolls along the waterfront promenade or relax at one of the many sidewalk cafes, sipping Turkish tea and watching the world go by.

The lifestyle in Kaş is both laid-back and culturally enriching. The town is steeped in history, with ancient ruins and amphitheaters offering glimpses into its storied past. Art and music festivals frequently fill the air with excitement, while local markets brim with fresh produce and handcrafted goods. Kaş is also a hub for outdoor enthusiasts, with opportunities for hiking along the Lycian Way, diving in crystal-clear waters, and exploring hidden caves and secluded beaches. This perfect blend of relaxation and adventure makes Kaş an enticing option for retirees looking to savor their golden years.

Culturally, Kaş offers a genuine taste of Turkish life. The locals are known for their friendliness, often inviting newcomers to share in traditional meals and festivities. The town’s weekly market is a social event, where residents gather to buy fresh fruits, vegetables, and spices while catching up on local news. Whether you're seeking the tranquility of a small town or the vibrancy of cultural events, Kaş provides a welcoming environment that celebrates both tradition and modernity.

## Practical Tips

For those considering retirement in Kaş, understanding the visa requirements is essential. Türkiye offers a long-term residence permit for retirees, which can be obtained through a straightforward application process. It's advisable to consult with a local immigration expert to ensure you have all the necessary documentation in order. Safety in Kaş is generally high, with a low crime rate and a strong sense of community. The town is walkable, making it easy to explore without the need for a car, although public transportation is available for longer journeys.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/32562200/pexels-photo-32562200.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&fit=crop&h=627&w=1200)

Choosing the right neighborhood is crucial for a comfortable retirement. The town center offers easy access to shops, restaurants, and cultural sites, ideal for those who enjoy being in the heart of the action. For a quieter lifestyle, consider the nearby villages of Kalkan or Üzümlü, which provide a more rural setting with stunning views and a slower pace. Language can be a barrier, but many locals speak English, and there are opportunities to learn Turkish through community classes, which can enhance your experience and integration into local life.

Expat life in Kaş is vibrant and welcoming. There is a growing community of retirees from various countries, creating a diverse and supportive network. Social clubs and groups organize regular meet-ups, cultural outings, and volunteer opportunities, making it easy to make new friends and stay active. Whether you're interested in art, history, or outdoor activities, you'll find plenty of like-minded individuals to share your interests and experiences with.

## Cost & Healthcare

Living in Kaş offers an affordable yet high-quality lifestyle, perfect for retirees on a budget of $2,000 to $5,000 per month. The cost of living is relatively low compared to other European destinations. Rent for a comfortable apartment in the town center can range from $500 to $1,000 per month, while properties with sea views or in quieter areas are slightly higher. Daily expenses such as groceries, dining out, and entertainment are also budget-friendly, allowing you to enjoy a rich and fulfilling lifestyle without financial strain.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/24609381/pexels-photo-24609381.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&fit=crop&h=627&w=1200)

Transportation in Kaş is convenient and economical. The town’s compact size makes walking the preferred mode of travel, but local buses and dolmuş (shared minibuses) provide affordable options for exploring the surrounding areas. For those who wish to travel further afield, Antalya’s international airport is a few hours drive away, offering connections to major cities across Europe and beyond.

Healthcare in Türkiye is of a high standard, with modern facilities and well-trained medical professionals. Kaş has several clinics and pharmacies, ensuring easy access to basic healthcare services. For more specialized care, nearby cities such as Antalya offer comprehensive medical centers and hospitals. Many doctors speak English, and private health insurance is recommended to cover any medical expenses. Overall, retirees can expect reliable and affordable healthcare, providing peace of mind as they enjoy their retirement in this beautiful region.

Retiring in Kaş offers a harmonious blend of relaxation, adventure, and community. With its stunning natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and welcoming atmosphere, this hidden gem on Türkiye’s Mediterranean coast is a perfect choice for those seeking an idyllic retirement destination.
