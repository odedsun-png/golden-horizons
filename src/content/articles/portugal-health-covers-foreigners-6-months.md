## Foreign Residents and the SNS: How to Access Public Health Care in Portugal

Foreign residents in Portugal can access the SNS by obtaining a user number, known as the "número de utente." This number is essential for accessing public healthcare services. Both EU/EEA nationals and third-country nationals with legal residence are eligible. Registration at a local health center is the first step to enrollment.

The SNS provides a range of services, including primary care, pediatrics, and nursing. While the system is publicly funded, co-payments are required for certain services. For example, a GP visit might cost between €5 and €20, depending on the service and any applicable exemptions.

## Enrollment Steps and Practical Considerations for Retirees

To enroll in the SNS, foreign residents must visit a local health center with their residence permit, passport, and proof of address. The process involves obtaining a health user number, which grants access to SNS services. It's important to confirm enrollment status and expected wait times for appointments.

Retirees should consider the proximity of health centers and hospitals when choosing a residence. Language services for English-speaking patients may vary, so it's advisable to inquire about available support at local facilities.

## Language and Local Health Center Proximity

Language can be a barrier for non-Portuguese speakers. While many health professionals speak English, it's beneficial to verify language services at your local health center. Proximity to healthcare facilities is also crucial, especially for retirees who may require frequent visits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I enroll in Portugal’s SNS if I am a non-EU citizen with a residence permit?

Yes. Foreign citizens with legal residence in Portugal can obtain an SNS user number and access SNS services; enrollment steps include registering at a health center. (gov.pt)

### What is the process to get a SNS user number?

The process typically involves visiting a health center or hospital to obtain a health user number, which then allows access to SNS services. (gov.pt)

### Are foreigners charged for SNS services?

SNS patients incur co-payments; foreigners access on the same terms, with payments according to SNS rules and exemptions. (ers.pt)

### Can I use EHIC for healthcare in Portugal?

EHIC can be used during temporary stays in EU countries if the person is enrolled and insured; for Portugal-based residents, SNS enrollment is the route to care. (gov.pt)

### Where can I find official guidance on enrollment?

Official guidance is available at gov.pt (Migrant health in Portugal) and the ERS SNS FAQ pages. (gov.pt; ers.pt)
