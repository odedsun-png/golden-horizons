# Ovos Moles: Aveiro's Sweets Served in Little Seashell Wafers

## What Ovos Moles Taste Like

The filling is soft, creamy and very sweet. It is made from fresh egg yolks and sugar cooked to a syrup stage, and some versions add chocolate. The wafer is thin and light, so the texture is almost all filling.

The wafers are shaped like sea things: clams, fish, small boats, conch shells. Some are coated in a sugar glaze that makes them opaque and firmer.

## The Story Behind the Sweet

Ovos moles trace back to the convents of Aveiro. A Portuguese reference says the nuns used the egg whites to starch their habits, and the leftover yolks became sweets. After the convents closed, women who had been taught by the nuns kept making them.

Today the name is protected by the European Union as a Protected Geographical Indication. One Portuguese source calls it the first Portuguese sweet to earn that status. We could not confirm the year, so we do not give one.

## How and Where to Buy Them

Ovos moles are traditionally sold in small painted wooden barrels decorated with moliceiro boats, and in little ceramic cups. Vendors in regional costume once sold them at the Aveiro train station on the Porto to Lisbon line.

Look for a shop in central Aveiro that makes them on site. Ask which pieces are fresh today and how long the shop keeps them. We did not verify prices or opening hours for any named shop, so call or check before you walk across town.

## Eating Them Safely at 65 and Over

Ovos moles are an egg-based sweet. The CDC lists adults 65 and older among the groups at higher risk from Salmonella, and the European Food Safety Authority says eggs are among the foods where Salmonella is most often found. We could not verify how each shop prepares its filling, so buy from a busy shop with fast turnover and ask how long pieces are kept. The CDC also recommends hepatitis A vaccination for Portugal travelers who plan to eat street food, so check your own vaccine plan with your doctor before you go.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are ovos moles made of?

Ovos moles are made of egg yolks whisked with sugar at about 1 part sugar to 2 parts yolk by weight, sometimes with chocolate added, inside a thin wafer of rice starch or wheat flour ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovos_moles))

### What shapes do the wafers come in?

The wafers come in at least 5 sea shapes: clams, fish, small boats, shells and conchs ([pt.wikipedia.org](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovos_moles_de_Aveiro))

### Is Portugal safe for older travelers?

Portugal is Level 1, Exercise Normal Precautions, in an advisory dated December 22, 2025, with a warning about petty theft in crowded tourist areas ([cadataapi.state.gov](https://cadataapi.state.gov/api/TravelAdvisories))

### Are eggs a food safety concern for adults over 65?

The CDC lists adults 65 and older among the groups at higher risk of Salmonella infection, so ask how a shop prepares and stores its pieces ([cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov/salmonella/about/index.html))

### Do I need a vaccine to eat street food in Portugal?

The CDC recommends hepatitis A vaccination for travelers who plan to eat street food in Portugal, and says unvaccinated travelers 60 and older may get hepatitis B vaccine before travel ([wwwnc.cdc.gov](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/portugal))
