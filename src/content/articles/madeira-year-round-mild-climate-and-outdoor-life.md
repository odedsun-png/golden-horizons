## The Planning-Budget Reality

Madeira's cost of living is variable, with significant differences based on location and lifestyle. Rent for a 1-bedroom apartment in Funchal typically ranges from €1,000 to €1,500+, while groceries and dining costs vary. Healthcare and taxes should be budgeted separately, as they depend on residency status and personal circumstances.

## The Residency Reality

Portugal offers several residency options for retirees, including retiree visas and the Golden Visa program. While Madeira follows national residency rules, individual eligibility and requirements must be verified through official channels.

## Practical Comparison

Madeira's mild climate and residency pathways make it a viable winter-retirement option compared to other European destinations. However, affordability remains variable, and healthcare specifics require careful consideration.
