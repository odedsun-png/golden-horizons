## Who This May Fit

**Worth testing if:**
- You prioritize a mild climate and outdoor activities year-round.

## The Trade-Off

**What you may gain:**
- Consistently mild climate enabling outdoor activity year-round.
- Active expat community and potential lower living costs relative to some Western European cities.
- Established residency routes in Portugal.

**What you may give up:**
- Island-specific travel logistics for specialized care.
- Variable housing costs by district and dependence on imported goods.
- Limited off-island access for some services; residency/legal processes require official guidance.
