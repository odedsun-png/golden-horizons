# Safest Areas for Retirement in Montenegro

Montenegro, with its stunning Adriatic coastline, charming medieval towns, and breathtaking mountainous landscapes, has become an increasingly popular destination for retirees seeking an affordable yet enriching European lifestyle. Known for its friendly locals, rich history, and diverse cultural influences, Montenegro offers a unique blend of Eastern European and Mediterranean living. For retirees considering a move, this small Balkan country presents a range of safe and welcoming areas that cater to different lifestyle preferences and budgets.

## Why Retire Here

Montenegro's allure lies in its picturesque settings and its ability to cater to both tranquil and adventurous lifestyles. The country's natural beauty is complemented by its mild Mediterranean climate, providing warm summers and mild winters, ideal for those looking to escape harsher climates. The coastal areas, in particular, offer stunning views and a relaxed atmosphere, perfect for retirees seeking a peaceful retreat.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos//pexels-photo-.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

First and foremost, Montenegro boasts a rich cultural heritage. The country's history is a tapestry of various civilizations, including the Romans, Ottomans, and Venetians, each leaving its mark on the architecture, cuisine, and traditions. This cultural diversity enriches the everyday life of residents, offering a vibrant and engaging environment for retirees to explore and enjoy.

Moreover, Montenegro is known for its safety and welcoming community. The crime rate is relatively low, and the locals are known for their hospitality and friendliness towards foreigners. This sense of safety and community makes it an ideal place for retirees looking to settle down in a peaceful and supportive environment. Additionally, English is widely spoken, particularly in tourist areas, making it easier for newcomers to communicate and integrate into local life.

## Practical Tips

When considering retirement in Montenegro, it's essential to research and select the area that best suits your lifestyle and preferences. The coastal towns of Kotor, Budva, and Herceg Novi are popular choices among retirees for their scenic beauty and vibrant local culture. Each of these towns offers a unique charm, from Kotor's UNESCO-listed old town to Budva's lively beaches and nightlife.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos//pexels-photo-.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

For those seeking a quieter lifestyle, the inland town of Cetinje offers a serene environment rich with history and culture. As Montenegro's former royal capital, Cetinje is home to numerous museums, monasteries, and cultural sites, providing a tranquil yet culturally rich setting for retirees. Additionally, the town's higher elevation offers a cooler climate, which can be a refreshing change from the warmer coastal areas.

Before making the move, it's advisable to spend some time visiting different areas in Montenegro to get a feel for each location. Renting a property for a few months can provide valuable insight into the local lifestyle and help determine if it's the right fit. Engaging with local expat communities can also offer practical advice and support during the transition to retirement in Montenegro.

## Cost & Healthcare

One of the most appealing aspects of retiring in Montenegro is its affordability. With a budget range of $2000 to $5000 per month, retirees can enjoy a comfortable lifestyle that includes housing, dining, and entertainment. Costs can vary significantly depending on the location, with coastal areas generally being more expensive than inland towns. However, even in popular areas like Kotor or Budva, the cost of living remains reasonable compared to Western European standards.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos//pexels-photo-.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

Housing costs are a significant factor to consider. Renting a one-bedroom apartment in the city center can range from $400 to $800 per month, while outside the city center, prices can be even lower. For those interested in purchasing property, Montenegro offers a range of options from modern apartments to traditional stone houses, with prices varying based on location and property size.

Healthcare in Montenegro is another crucial consideration for retirees. The country provides a public healthcare system that is accessible to residents, but many expats opt for private healthcare due to shorter waiting times and increased comfort. Private health insurance is relatively affordable and provides access to a higher standard of care. It's advisable to research and compare different health insurance plans to ensure comprehensive coverage that meets your needs. Furthermore, Montenegro is in the process of improving its healthcare infrastructure, which bodes well for the future quality of care available to residents.

In conclusion, Montenegro offers a captivating combination of natural beauty, cultural richness, and affordability, making it an attractive destination for retirees. By choosing the right location, understanding the cost of living, and ensuring adequate healthcare coverage, retirees can enjoy a fulfilling and secure retirement in this enchanting Balkan country.
