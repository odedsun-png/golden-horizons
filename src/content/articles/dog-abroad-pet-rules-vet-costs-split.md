## Your Dog in Split: Entry Rules, Vet Care Basics, and Everyday Life

### Pet Entry Requirements
To bring your dog to Croatia, you'll need a microchip and a rabies vaccination documented in an internationally recognized pet passport. Croatia follows EU guidelines, so ensure your dog meets these standards before traveling. Check with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs ([mvep.gov.hr](https://mvep.gov.hr/non-commercial-movement-of-pets/140781)) for the latest requirements.

### Veterinary Care Costs
Routine veterinary visits in Split typically range from EUR45-90 (~$50-100), covering vaccinations and check-ups. Emergency care can be more expensive, so it's wise to budget for unexpected vet visits. Contact local clinics for precise pricing.

### Daily Life with a Dog
Split's walkable neighborhoods and pet-friendly parks make it ideal for dog owners. The Riva promenade and Marjan Hill are popular spots for daily walks. Be mindful of leash rules and seasonal tourist crowds, which can affect your routine.
