# Costa Rica - Safest Towns Like Atenas for Retirement — A Local's Perspective

Atenas, a town in Costa Rica, boasts a population of approximately 27,000 and is known for having one of the best climates in the world, according to National Geographic. This small town, located about 25 kilometers west of the capital San José, is often highlighted for its safety and relaxed lifestyle, making it a popular choice for retirees. In this article, we will review Atenas and other similarly safe towns in Costa Rica to help you determine if they align with your retirement goals.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want a safe, mild climate with a welcoming community.
- **Monthly reality:** $1,500–$2,500 for comfortable living, including rent, utilities, and groceries.
- **Biggest advantage:** A strong sense of community and safety.
- **Biggest warning:** Limited access to some specialized healthcare services.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Atenas: The Climate and Community

Atenas is renowned for its mild climate, which averages between 70°F to 80°F year-round. This town is situated in the Central Valley, providing easy access to both the active capital and beautiful Pacific beaches. Atenas is characterized by its friendly atmosphere and active expat community, which makes integration easier for newcomers.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1550853123-b81beb0b1449?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxMXx8Q29zdGElMjBSaWNhfGVufDB8MHx8fDE3NzgxODIzNzd8MA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

The town center, with its local markets and small businesses, offers a glimpse into Costa Rican daily life. The weekly farmers' market is a highlight, providing fresh produce and an opportunity to mingle with locals. However, while Atenas is generally safe, petty theft can occur, especially in crowded areas, so it's wise to remain vigilant.

## Grecia: Tradition and Modernity

Grecia, located about 20 kilometers from Atenas, is another town that provides a combination of a safe environment for retirees. Known for its distinctive red metal church, Iglesia de la Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes, Grecia combines traditional Costa Rican culture with some modern amenities. The town has a population of around 76,000 and provides a more urban feel compared to Atenas.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579380231498-e45d45213373?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8Q29zdGElMjBSaWNhfGVufDB8MHx8fDE3NzgxODIzNzd8MA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Healthcare in Grecia is more accessible than in smaller towns, with Hospital San Francisco de Asís and several private clinics offering a range of services. However, for highly specialized care, a trip to San José might be necessary. Public transport is available, but owning a car can make day-to-day life more convenient, especially for exploring the scenic surroundings.

## San Ramón: Nature and Safety

San Ramón, often referred to as the "City of Poets," is located about 45 kilometers from San José and is known for its cultural heritage and natural beauty. The town is surrounded by lush landscapes and is home to several parks and reserves, making it ideal for retirees who enjoy outdoor activities.

The local community is welcoming, and the crime rate is relatively low, contributing to a sense of security. The town hosts the University of Costa Rica's regional campus, which adds to the cultural vibrancy. However, the hilly terrain can be challenging for those with mobility issues, and while local healthcare facilities are available, specialized treatments may require travel to larger cities.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

When planning retirement in Costa Rica, healthcare is a vital aspect to evaluate. While Atenas and nearby towns offer basic healthcare services, access to specialized care often requires a trip to San José. Securing private health insurance to cover any gaps in the public system is recommended.

Visa requirements for retirees typically involve the Pensionado Residency, which requires proof of a minimum monthly income from a pension or Social Security. Tax obligations can be complex, as Costa Rica taxes worldwide income, so consulting a tax professional familiar with both U.S. and Costa Rican systems is essential.

Safety in towns like Atenas, Grecia, and San Ramón is generally high, but common sense precautions, such as securing your home and belongings, are recommended. Walkability varies, with some areas being more pedestrian-friendly than others, so evaluate transportation options based on your mobility needs.

## Common Mistakes Retirees Should Avoid

- Assuming all healthcare needs can be met locally without checking specific services.
- Overlooking the need for private health insurance.
- Failing to understand Costa Rica's tax obligations on worldwide income.
- Underestimating the importance of learning basic Spanish for daily interactions.
- Ignoring petty crime risks and not securing personal belongings.
- Forgetting to verify rental agreements and housing conditions.
- Overlooking transportation needs, especially if living outside urban centers.

## Before You Move Checklist

- Verify visa or residency rules, such as Pensionado Residency.
- Confirm healthcare access and consider private insurance.
- Understand Costa Rican tax obligations and consult a tax professional.
- Check rent and lease agreements thoroughly.
- Assess walkability and transportation options.
- Evaluate safety and emergency access in your chosen town.
- Consider language barriers and plan to learn basic Spanish.
- Research local community activities and support networks.
- Ensure proximity to essential services, such as hospitals and markets.
- Plan for initial accommodation during your transition period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cost of living in Atenas?

Living in Atenas typically costs between $1,500 and $2,500 per month, depending on lifestyle choices. This budget includes rent, utilities, groceries, and some leisure activities.

### How safe is Grecia for retirees?

Grecia is considered safe for retirees, with a welcoming community and low crime rates. However, it's always wise to take standard safety precautions to protect your belongings.

### Can I access healthcare easily in San Ramón?

San Ramón offers basic healthcare facilities and clinics. For more specialized care, you may need to travel to San José, so having private insurance can be beneficial.
