### Residency Is a Separate Conversation

A scouting stay and a long-term move are different commitments. Before you build a retirement plan around Arequipa, speak directly with Peru’s Superintendencia Nacional de Migraciones about the current residence route that fits an American retiree.

Bring your passport, proof of income, and the address where you expect to stay when requesting current instructions. The $235-$360 rent range is useful for planning, but it is not a residency requirement.

A furnished lease can make the first 30 days easier, particularly in Yanahuara, Miraflores, or Centro. Ask the landlord, in writing if possible, what is included: water, internet, furniture, building fees, and the length of the agreement.

### The Evening Budget Includes Feeling Comfortable Getting Home

The U.S. State Department’s September 4, 2026 advisory places Peru at Level 2: exercise increased caution because of crime and unrest. The advisory also identifies the Colombia-Peru border area in Loreto and the VRAEM area as Do Not Travel areas.

For your life in Arequipa, practical habits matter more than dramatic ones. Use a registered taxi or app ride after dark, keep your phone out of sight in crowded areas, and ask your landlord which routes they use after 8 p.m. A taxi beginning near $2.03 can be a worthwhile evening expense.

Arequipa is not the restricted border or VRAEM region, but the national guidance still belongs in your planning. Housing may fit the budget. Healthcare is where the decision becomes more personal.

## Healthcare May Cost Less—But It Needs Its Own Plan

A short private doctor visit is referenced around $21, making a stop at Clínica Arequipa during a scouting stay a useful errand rather than an abstract concern. Ask for the current outpatient price list, then make the same inquiry at another private clinic near the neighborhood you are considering.

The key questions are not only about price. Ask what a same-week appointment costs, whether English-speaking staff are available, how prescriptions are filled, and when payment is expected. If you take regular medications or see specialists, bring a written list with you.

Clínica Arequipa is one starting point for a self-pay price inquiry. Request current estimates for a routine consultation, labs, imaging, and repeat prescriptions. Your $1,600 plan can support an enjoyable daily life, but routine care and an unexpected hospital bill should not share the same mental category—or the same small monthly cushion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Arequipa affordable for a retiree on $1,600 a month?

It can be, particularly for a single renter who cooks at home and chooses housing carefully. A one-bedroom averages about $355-$360 in the center and about $235-$240 outside it, while a structured core plan falls around $1,025-$1,340 before medical coverage. That can leave $260-$575 each month, but furnished-rental terms and medical costs can change the math quickly. (Numbeo, October 2026)

### What should I ask about residency before renting in Arequipa?

Ask Peru’s Superintendencia Nacional de Migraciones which current long-stay route applies to you and what documents it requires before committing to a 6- or 12-month lease. Bring your passport, proof of income, and your expected local address when you ask for instructions. The $235-$360 rent range is useful for planning, but it is separate from permission to stay. (Expat.com)

### How should I budget for healthcare in Arequipa?

Keep healthcare outside the basic $1,025-$1,340 living estimate until you have current quotes. A short private visit is referenced around $21; ask Clínica Arequipa and another private provider about consultations, labs, imaging, and prescription costs. If you use regular medications, bring a written list and ask how much a refill costs before you settle on a monthly medical reserve. (Expatistan, October 2026)

### What is the current U.S. safety guidance for Peru?

The U.S. State Department lists Peru at Level 2, meaning exercise increased caution, in an advisory dated September 4, 2026. It identifies the Colombia-Peru border area in Loreto and the VRAEM area as Do Not Travel zones. Arequipa is not in those zones, but using registered taxis after dark and keeping valuables out of sight are sensible daily habits. (U.S. State Department)

### Why can the same Arequipa budget change from month to month?

Local prices are paid in Peruvian sol while your retirement income is likely in U.S. dollars. October 2026 exchange-rate history placed one sol at roughly $0.29, so currency movement can alter how a $235 apartment or a $2.50 coffee feels against your income. Review your actual rent, grocery, and taxi spending after the first 30 days rather than relying only on a planning estimate. (Exchange-Rates.org, October 2026)
