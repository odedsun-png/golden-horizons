# Spain Dinner Often Served Until 11:30 PM

> **Did you know?** In Spain, dinner is commonly served between 8:00 PM and 11:30 PM, with late dining being a cultural norm.

Spain is known for its vibrant nightlife and relaxed pace, and this extends to its dining habits. In many Spanish cities, especially during the warm summer months, it's perfectly normal to sit down for dinner as late as 11:30 PM. This late dining culture is a delightful surprise for many visitors who are used to earlier meal times.

The tradition of eating late is deeply rooted in Spanish culture, influenced by the country's climate and social customs. During the hot summer days, the cooler evening hours become the perfect time to enjoy a leisurely meal. As you stroll through the bustling streets of Madrid or Barcelona, you'll notice restaurants filled with locals and tourists alike, savoring their meals well into the night.

So, if you find yourself in Spain, embrace the local lifestyle and enjoy a late-night dinner under the stars!
