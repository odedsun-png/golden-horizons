# Vietnam - Renting Apartments and Condos in Vietnam — A Local's Perspective
**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING — VERIFY RESIDENCY

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** On Numbeo's November 2026 data, a one-bedroom outside the center rents for about $301 in Ho Chi Minh City, $292 in Hanoi and $359 in Da Nang, and in the center for about $621, $440 and $555. Adding one person's other costs gives monthly totals of about $784, $751 and $823 outside the center. The catch: these are user-submitted averages for whole cities, not districts, and we could not confirm a Vietnamese retirement visa, so the legal right to stay long enough to sign a lease is the real question.

A cappuccino costs about $1.77 in Ho Chi Minh City, $1.90 in Hanoi and $1.67 in Da Nang. Everyday prices are low, and the decision that matters is the apartment and the visa behind it.

## Retirement Snapshot

**Primary decision:**  
Which city to rent in, and whether you can stay long enough to make a lease worthwhile.

**Best fit:**  
Hanoi has the lowest outside-center total on this data. Ho Chi Minh City has the highest center rent. Da Nang sits between them, but its page includes comments saying prices are outdated.

**Biggest warning:**  
Residency. We did not find or confirm a Vietnamese retirement visa. Secondary sources describe a tourist e-visa of up to 90 days for U.S. citizens, which is not a long-stay plan.

**Residency:**  
Check the official e-visa portal and your nearest Vietnamese consulate before you sign anything. Our check of evisa.gov.vn did not load.

**Healthcare:**  
Original Medicare generally does not pay for care outside the United States. Ask clinics for written self-pay prices.

**Safety:**  
The U.S. State Department rates Vietnam Level 1, Exercise Normal Precautions (June 23, 2026).

## What a Rental Has to Cover

Numbeo lists these one-bedroom rents, converted to dollars at the rate implied by its own single-person totals.

- Ho Chi Minh City (110 contributors): about $301 outside the center, $621 in the center.
- Hanoi (88 contributors): about $292 outside the center, $440 in the center.
- Da Nang (77 contributors): about $359 outside the center, $555 in the center.

One person's monthly costs without rent are about $483 in Ho Chi Minh City, $459 in Hanoi and $464 in Da Nang. Add outside-center rent and you reach the totals in the short answer.

Utilities for an apartment of about 915 square feet run near $102 in Ho Chi Minh City and $85 in Hanoi, and $86 in Da Nang. Broadband is about $9 to $10 everywhere. A monthly transit pass is about $12 in Ho Chi Minh City, $11 in Hanoi and $7 in Da Nang.

These are city averages. They do not tell you what a condo in a specific district costs, so use them to compare cities and then collect real quotes.

## Visa and Healthcare: The Limits on a Lease

A lease is only useful if you can legally stay for its term. Secondary sources describe a 90-day e-visa for U.S. citizens, applied for on the official portal at evisa.gov.vn, and none of the pages we saw mentioned a retirement visa. We could not open the official portal, so confirm the current visa options, fees and stay limits with the Vietnamese embassy or a consulate before you commit to a 12-month lease.

Healthcare needs its own plan. Original Medicare generally does not pay for medical services outside the United States. For comparison, the 2026 Part B deductible at home is $283 a year. Ask clinics in each city for written self-pay prices and compare with your costs at home.

## The Golden Horizons 30-Day Test

1. View two apartments in each city, one central and one outside the center, and ask what the rent includes.
2. Ask for the last three utility bills for each apartment.
3. Read the lease line by line and ask what happens if you must leave early.
4. Ask the Vietnamese consulate or embassy in writing which visa fits your planned stay, and keep the reply.
5. Ask two clinics in your chosen city for written self-pay prices for a check-up and a specialist visit.
6. Spend three ordinary weekdays in the area: one coffee, one lunch, one grocery trip, one ride. Record what your routine costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Vietnam?

A one-bedroom outside the center is about $301 in Ho Chi Minh City, $292 in Hanoi and $359 in Da Nang, and in the center about $621, $440 and $555. These are citywide user-submitted averages from 110, 88 and 77 contributors. ([numbeo.com](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Ho-Chi-Minh-City))

### Is there a retirement visa for Vietnam?

We could not confirm one: secondary sources describe a 90-day e-visa for U.S. citizens, and our check of the official portal did not load. Ask a Vietnamese consulate which visa fits a stay of several months before signing a lease. ([evisa.gov.vn](https://evisa.gov.vn/))

### Does Medicare cover me in Vietnam?

No for routine care: Original Medicare generally does not pay for medical services outside the United States. Ask 2 clinics for written self-pay prices and plan for private insurance. ([aarp.org](https://www.aarp.org/medicare/does-medicare-cover-me-outside-the-us/))

### What does Medicare cost at home in 2026?

The Part B deductible is $283 a year and the Part A deductible is $1,736 per hospital benefit period. Compare that with private care abroad before you move. ([medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/medicare-costs))

### Is Vietnam safe for older travelers?

The State Department rates Vietnam Level 1, Exercise Normal Precautions, as of June 23, 2026. Read the full advisory for regional notes before you book a scouting trip. ([cadataapi.state.gov](https://cadataapi.state.gov/api/TravelAdvisories))
