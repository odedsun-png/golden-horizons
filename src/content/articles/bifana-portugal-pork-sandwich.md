# Bifana: Portugal's $3 Pork Sandwich Lunch

**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING — VERIFY COSTS

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** A bifana is a pork sandwich on a bread roll, and one travel guide puts 2026 Lisbon prices at about $2.70 to $4.40. At O Trevo, a standing-room counter in Chiado, the same guide says it runs about $2.70 to $3.30. The catch is that prices vary by counter and no official price list exists, so treat every figure as a starting point. Portugal holds a Level 1 U.S. State Department advisory, the lowest of 4 levels.

## What a Bifana Is and How Locals Eat It

A bifana is a pork sandwich. The pork is sliced thin, seasoned with garlic and spices, and served in a bread roll. Recipes and toppings differ by cook, so ask what is in yours. Some counters offer mustard or hot sauce on the side.

Locals often eat one standing at a counter, with a coffee or a beer. A bifana is a quick meal, not a sit-down lunch. If a place has a short menu and a line of regulars, that is usually a good sign.

## Where to Find a Bifana in Lisbon

The guide we checked names one counter, O Trevo, a tiny standing spot on Praça Luís de Camões in Chiado that is known for its bifana. It also says cervejarias (beer halls) in Baixa and Cais do Sodré serve them. We did not verify current menus at any of these places, so confirm the price on the board before you order.

## Cost and Ordering Tips

At the guide's range, a bifana plus a coffee is a lunch for under $7. Tourlane lists a bica, the local espresso, at $1.00 to $1.50. Prices are higher at stalls in busy tourist areas, so walk a few streets away from the main squares. Ask whether extras such as cheese or an egg cost more.

## Practical Comparison

**What to check next:** Read the price board before ordering, and ask if the price is for the sandwich alone or includes a drink.

### Bifana versus a pastel de nata snack

**What the evidence says:** Tourlane lists a pastel de nata at $1.50 to $2.00, so a bifana and a tart together can be a small lunch for under $8.

**What to check next:** Compare the printed prices at two counters on the same street.

### Eating for health at 65 and older

**What the evidence says:** A fried or grilled pork sandwich is high in salt and fat. Medicare.gov says Original Medicare usually does not cover health care outside the U.S., with a few exceptions, so food-related illness abroad is a cost you would likely carry.

**What to check next:** If you take medication with food, ask your doctor how a salty lunch fits your plan. Ask your Medigap insurer, by phone, whether it covers emergency care abroad and what the limit is.

## The Trade-Off

**What you may give up:** A single sandwich price says little about monthly costs, housing or healthcare. Prices at tourist counters can run higher than the ranges here, and we could not confirm any menu directly.
