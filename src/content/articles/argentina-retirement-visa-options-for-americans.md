# Argentina - Retirement Visa Options for Americans in Argentina

**Golden Horizons Status:** Use Official Pensionado route as the primary formal residency path for retirees who can demonstrate sufficient income/means and meet background checks.

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** Americans can pursue a temporary residence as a Pensionado (retiree) in Argentina, per official government guidance. Requirements include documenting pension income, police clearance, medical certification, and health coverage. Argentina’s healthcare system is three-tiered, with access for foreigners depending on residency status and provincial rules.
