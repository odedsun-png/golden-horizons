## How to Get a Croatian Temporary Stay Permit in Rovinj: The 3-Step Path

### Step 1: Gather Required Documents
To apply for a temporary stay permit in Rovinj, you'll need to gather several key documents. These include a valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means (typically around EUR920 (~$1,000) per month), health insurance coverage, and a clear purpose for your stay, such as retirement. Ensure all documents are translated into Croatian and notarized.

### Step 2: Submit Your Application
Head to the local police administration office in Rovinj to submit your application. The office will guide you through the process, which includes filling out forms and paying an application fee of approximately EUR90 (~$100). Be prepared for a processing time of up to 90 days, during which your documents will be reviewed.

### Step 3: Plan for Living Costs
While waiting for your permit, plan your budget. Rent in Rovinj ranges from EUR460-650 (~$500-700) for a one-bedroom apartment. Groceries and utilities will add another EUR180-275 (~$200-300) monthly. Ensure you have private health insurance or check eligibility for public healthcare enrollment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What documents are needed for a temporary stay permit in Rovinj?
You'll need a valid passport, proof of financial means (around EUR920 (~$1,000) per month), health insurance, and a purpose for your stay. Ensure documents are translated into Croatian and notarized. (gov.hr)

### How long does it take to process a temporary stay permit?
Processing can take up to 90 days. It's crucial to have all documents in order to avoid delays. (mup.gov.hr)

### What are the living costs in Rovinj?
Expect to pay EUR460-650 (~$500-700) for rent, EUR140-185 (~$150-200) for groceries, and EUR90-140 (~$100-150) for utilities monthly. Dining out costs about EUR9-14 (~$10-15) per meal. (numbeo.com)

### Is private health insurance necessary in Croatia?
While public healthcare is available, private insurance is recommended for comprehensive coverage. (investcroatia.gov.hr)

### Can I use my U.S. Social Security benefits in Croatia?
Yes, but consider the exchange rate and local cost of living to ensure your benefits cover your expenses. (ssa.gov)
