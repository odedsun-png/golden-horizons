## What daily life in Rovinj looks like for a 60–75-year-old retiree: neighborhoods, markets, healthcare proximity, and language
Rovinj offers a charming coastal lifestyle with a mix of historic and modern amenities. Neighborhoods like the Old Town provide a picturesque setting with cobblestone streets and local markets. Expect to spend around EUR470-750 (~$500-800) monthly on rent for a one-bedroom apartment. Groceries typically cost EUR190-280 (~$200-300) per month, with local markets offering fresh produce. Utilities, including internet, average about EUR95 (~$100) monthly. Healthcare is accessible, with both public and private options available. While Croatian is the official language, English is widely spoken, especially in tourist areas.
