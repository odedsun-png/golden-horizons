## Temporary Stay in Rovinj: Step-by-Step Residency Path for U.S. Retirees

To legally stay in Rovinj, Croatia, as a U.S. retiree, you'll need to secure a temporary residence permit. Here's a practical guide to navigating the process:

1. **Understand the Permit Requirements:**  
   The temporary residence permit is designed for third-country nationals, including U.S. retirees. You'll need to demonstrate sufficient income or savings, though exact thresholds require verification from official Croatian sources like [MUP](https://mup.gov.hr/stranci-281595/281595) and [gov.hr](https://gov.hr/?id=1211&url=print).

2. **Gather Necessary Documents:**  
   Prepare your passport, proof of financial means, health insurance, and accommodation details. The application process involves submitting these documents to the Croatian Ministry of the Interior.

3. **Submit Your Application:**  
   Applications are typically processed within a few months, but timelines can vary. It's crucial to apply well before your intended move to Rovinj. Check the latest processing times on [gov.hr](https://gov.hr/?id=1211&url=print).

## What the Rovinj Expat Life Actually Feels Like: Healthcare, Markets, and Daily Logistics

Living in Rovinj offers a blend of Mediterranean charm and practical amenities. Here's what to expect:

- **Healthcare Access:**  
  Rovinj is home to the Martin Horvat Orthopedic Hospital, providing essential healthcare services. Foreigners can access public health services, but enrollment specifics depend on your residency status and insurance. For more details, visit [Bolnica Rovinj](https://www.bolnica-rovinj.hr/en/naslovna-2/).

- **Local Markets and Daily Life:**  
  The Rovinj Market (Valdibora) is a central hub for fresh produce and local goods. It's a great place to gauge your monthly grocery budget and immerse yourself in local life. More information can be found on [Istra Tourism](https://www.istra.hr/pbdownloadf/rovinj_en.pdf).

- **Neighborhoods and Lifestyle:**  
  Rovinj's compact historic core is walkable, with easy access to markets, healthcare, and social venues. The town's bilingual culture (Croatian and Italian) adds a unique flavor to daily interactions.
