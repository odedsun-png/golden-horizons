# Pura Vida Used as a Greeting in Costa Rica

> **Did you know?** The phrase Pura Vida can be heard echoing throughout Costa Rica and is used as a greeting.

In Costa Rica, the expression "Pura Vida" is more than just a saying—it's a way of life. Translating to "pure life," this colloquial phrase is a staple in the everyday vocabulary of Costa Ricans. Whether you're meeting someone for the first time or catching up with an old friend, "Pura Vida" serves as a warm and welcoming greeting.

But "Pura Vida" goes beyond just a simple hello. It's an expression of happiness and contentment, embodying a lifestyle choice to live life to the fullest. This philosophy encourages nurturing the mind, body, and soul, making it a central part of the Costa Rican way of life.

So, next time you find yourself in Costa Rica, don't be surprised to hear "Pura Vida" echoing around you. It's a reminder to embrace the joy and simplicity of life, Costa Rican style.
