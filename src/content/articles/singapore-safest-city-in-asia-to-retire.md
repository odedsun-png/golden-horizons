**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟠 HIGH HURDLE

## The Safety Reality

Singapore consistently ranks as the safest city in Asia, celebrated for its low crime rates and robust law enforcement. According to multiple 2026 safety indices, including the Remitly Immigration Index and Crossborders, Singapore is recognized for its safety across various domains such as digital, infrastructure, and health. The U.S. Department of State classifies Singapore as Level 1, advising travelers to exercise normal precautions.

While the general safety environment is excellent, retirees should be aware of financial scams that specifically target older adults. In 2025, the average loss per scam victim was approximately SGD 37,000, highlighting the need for vigilance.

## The Residency Reality

For baseline U.S. retirees, Singapore presents significant challenges in terms of legal residency. There is no dedicated retirement visa available. Long-term stay options generally require family sponsorship, employment, or substantial investment—conditions that most retirees do not meet. The Long-Term Visit Pass, for example, is family-linked and requires a local sponsor, such as a Singapore citizen or permanent resident.

![Singapore housing](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1514623471391-d83c3ff44cd0?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxfHxTaW5nYXBvcmUlMjBob3VzaW5nfGVufDB8MHwxfHwxNzg2NDU5MzMwfDA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Work-related passes, like the Employment Pass or Tech.Pass, demand active employment and high salaries, which are not applicable to retirees. Therefore, without family ties or employment, retirees face high hurdles for legal residency.

## Healthcare Reality

Singapore's healthcare system is highly regarded, but public healthcare benefits and subsidies are limited to citizens and permanent residents. Foreign retirees must rely on private insurance and pay out-of-pocket for medical services. While the quality of healthcare is high, the costs can be significant for those without access to public subsidies.

Retirees should thoroughly investigate private healthcare options and costs before deciding to relocate. The Ministry of Health Singapore provides detailed information on healthcare schemes and subsidies, which are crucial for understanding the financial implications of healthcare in Singapore.
