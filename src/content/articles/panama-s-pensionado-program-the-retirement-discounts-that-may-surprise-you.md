## Residency Eligibility and Threshold
Panama’s Pensionado program is a compelling option for retirees seeking a simpler, more affordable lifestyle. To qualify, you need a verified lifetime pension of at least $1,000 per month, with an additional $100 for each dependent. This requirement is clearly outlined in the official documents from Migración Panamá. The process begins with temporary residency, which can transition to permanent status, provided you maintain the necessary documentation, including proof of pension and background checks. Processing times can vary, so it's wise to consult with a local immigration attorney to ensure a smooth application process.

## Cost of Living and Discount Dynamics
Living in Panama City under the Pensionado program can be surprisingly affordable, especially with the discounts available to retirees. Rent for a one-bedroom apartment in neighborhoods like El Cangrejo ranges from $700 to $1,200, while groceries might cost between $180 and $260 monthly. Utilities and internet typically add another $90 to $180 to your budget. Dining out and everyday extras can range from $150 to $350, depending on your lifestyle.

The Pensionado program offers discounts on many services, including travel, dining, and utilities. However, these discounts are not universal and depend on the merchant. For example, a casual lunch might cost $6 to $12, and a coffee at a local café could be $1.50 to $2.50. Public transportation is also affordable, with local bus fares ranging from $0.25 to $0.50 per ride.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is the minimum monthly income required for Panama Pensionado residency?
The minimum income required is $1,000 per month as a lifetime pension, plus $100 for each dependent. This is per Migración Panamá documentation. (migracion.gob.pa)

### Do Pensionado discounts cover utilities and medical costs?
Discounts exist on many categories, including utilities and healthcare services, but apply at merchant discretion. (newsroompanama.com)

### Is Panama’s currency the U.S. dollar?
Yes, Panama uses the U.S. dollar as its de facto currency, simplifying budgeting for Americans. (numbeo.com)

### How long does it take to obtain Pensionado residency?
Processing times vary; typical steps include temporary status followed by permanent residency. (migracion.gob.pa)
