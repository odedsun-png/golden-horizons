# Realistic Cost Snapshot: What $2,000 a Month Gets an American Retiree in Asunción, Paraguay

**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟢 SOLID OPTION

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** A $2,000 monthly budget can cover a comfortable but watchful retirement in Asunción. A one-bedroom runs about $380-$750, groceries can land near $150-$250, utilities and internet about $65-$100, and a transit pass about $38. Add private GP visits of $15-$50 and ordinary meals of $6-$10, and the budget can work with room for choices outside the city center.

A bus ride for $0.58, coffee for a couple of dollars, and lunch for less than many Americans spend on a sandwich at home: that is the everyday arithmetic that makes Asunción worth a closer look. The question is not whether you can find a low-cost day here. You can. The real question is whether a $2,000 monthly retirement budget still feels roomy once you choose an apartment, set aside money for private care, and begin living an ordinary Tuesday rather than a vacation week.

**Biggest warning:**

## Private Care Is Accessible — But Keep a Reserve

A private GP appointment in Asunción is commonly quoted at $15-$50. For routine care, that can feel refreshingly manageable. But the reassuring number is only the beginning of the conversation: a hospital procedure may cost in the low thousands, and prices can differ substantially by service and provider.

Hospital Privado San Roque, Sanatorio Migone, and Sanatorio Italiano give you useful places to begin asking direct questions. Before choosing a neighborhood, request current consultation fees, laboratory pricing, specialist availability, and the details of any prepaid medical plan for which you may qualify.

For perspective, U.S. private health-insurance premiums for adults ages 60-64 can run $500-$900 per month. The cost of walking into a private clinic in Asunción may be far lower, but a sensible retirement plan still includes coverage questions, emergency planning, and cash set aside for care that does not fit neatly into a monthly budget.

## What an Ordinary Month Has to Cover

Housing is the decision that shapes everything else. A one-bedroom outside the city center is commonly around $380-$420 per month, while city-center housing can reach roughly $620-$750. That difference is not merely an address; it is the room you preserve for healthcare, dinners out, and a little flexibility when a bill arrives.

Luque is one area to include alongside outside-center Asunción and the city center when you begin comparing rentals. Ask every landlord what is included. Water, internet, and building charges can make a lower advertised rent look quite different by the end of the month.

The food math is gentle. Milk is about $1.30, bread about $1.40, and a dozen eggs about $2.40. A realistic solo grocery budget can stay near $150-$250, depending on what you buy and how often you choose to eat out.

Utilities and internet together commonly land around $65-$100. Public transport remains one of the easier line items: a monthly pass is around $38, while a single ride is about $0.58. Then come the pleasures that make a place feel livable rather than merely affordable: a casual meal for $6-$10, or a dinner for two at a mid-range restaurant for $25-$40 before drinks.

**A Typical Day in Asunción**

Picture a Tuesday like this: coffee at a local café for $1.20-$2.50, followed by a $0.58 bus ride and a casual lunch for $6-$10. On the way home, pick up milk for about $1.30 and eggs for about $2.40. In the evening, a modest dinner for two can come to $25-$40.

It is not an extravagant day. It is coffee, errands, a ride across town, and dinner without the feeling that every ordinary choice needs a calculator. The larger question is whether that easy daily rhythm still works once housing and healthcare enter the picture.

## Staying Long Enough to Know

Paraguay’s residency routes can involve documented income or savings, identity records, and other standard paperwork. Get the current requirements directly from Paraguay’s migration authorities before paying for translations, notarizations, or a long-term lease.

During a short stay, keep a simple folder of quotes: rent, a private doctor visit, a basic blood panel, internet, and mobile service. Ask whether each price applies to visitors, residents, or prepaid-plan members. Those details will tell you more about your likely monthly life than any broad promise about affordability.

For medical planning, contact Hospital Privado San Roque and Sanatorio Italiano before settling on an area. For housing, compare a furnished 30-day rental in Luque with one closer to the city center. The paperwork may require patience; the daily test is more immediate: grocery runs, buses, clinics, and the walk from your apartment to the nearest stop.
