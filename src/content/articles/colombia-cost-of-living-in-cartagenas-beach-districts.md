# Colombia - Cost of Living in Cartagena’s Beach Districts — A Local's Perspective

Cartagena, with its historic walled city and Caribbean coastline, might surprise you with its affordability. A two-bedroom apartment in the Bocagrande area—a district known for its skyscrapers and beaches—can cost as little as $700 per month to rent. This article will guide you through the financial landscape of living in Cartagena’s beach districts and help you decide if this coastal city aligns with your retirement plans.

## What $2,000/Month Actually Buys in Bocagrande

With a budget of $2,000 a month, you can live quite comfortably in Bocagrande, one of Cartagena's most sought-after neighborhoods. Bocagrande is a peninsula known for its modern apartments, shopping, and proximity to the beach. For around $700 to $1,000 monthly, you can rent a two-bedroom apartment with a view of the ocean. Utilities, including electricity, water, and internet, typically add an additional $100 to $150 to your monthly expenses.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1476514525535-07fb3b4ae5f1?w=1200)

Dining out is another area where your money stretches. A meal at a mid-range restaurant might cost around $30 for two people, while local eateries or street food can be significantly cheaper. Groceries at places like the Carulla supermarket can run about $200 to $300 per month, depending on your dietary preferences. Imported goods are pricier, so sticking to local produce and products can save you money.

Transportation is also reasonably priced. A 15-minute taxi ride within Bocagrande costs about $3 to $5, and using public transport is even more economical. However, one limitation to consider is that English is not widely spoken, so some basic Spanish skills can help in your day-to-day interactions.

## Healthcare Access in Cartagena: Hospitals and Clinics

Cartagena offers a variety of public and private healthcare facilities, with the private sector providing high-quality services at reasonable prices. The Medihelp Clinic in Bocagrande is a popular choice among expats for its comprehensive services and English-speaking staff. A consultation with a specialist at a private clinic might cost around $50 to $70, which is significantly lower than in the United States.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528360983277-13d401cdc186?w=1200)

For those considering health insurance, international plans such as Allianz or Cigna are available, though many expats choose to pay out-of-pocket due to the affordability of services. Prescription medication is also generally inexpensive and widely available at pharmacies throughout the city. However, check if any specific medications you require are available locally, as availability can vary.

Public hospitals like Hospital Universitario del Caribe provide basic services but can be crowded, so many opt for private care for more immediate attention. An interesting aspect of healthcare in Cartagena is the availability of home visit services, where doctors can come to your residence for a consultation at a slightly higher fee. This can be especially convenient for retirees who prefer to avoid the activity level of busy clinics.

## A Different Pace: Lifestyle and Community in El Laguito

El Laguito, a small neighborhood adjacent to Bocagrande, offers a slightly different pace of life. Known for its residential high-rises and proximity to the Hilton Hotel, El Laguito is quieter and more laid-back compared to the busy Bocagrande. The cost of living here is similar, with apartment rentals ranging from $600 to $900 per month.

This area is particularly appealing if you're looking for a strong sense of community. The neighborhood has a mix of locals and expats, which makes it easier to form connections. Regular community events and gatherings at the local parks or the beach are common, fostering a welcoming atmosphere.

One surprise for many is the emphasis on outdoor activities and wellness. Yoga classes on the beach and group exercise sessions in local parks are frequent and affordable, often costing around $5 per session. This focus on health and fitness is a significant draw for those looking to maintain an active lifestyle.

While El Laguito offers a peaceful environment, it does have its limitations. The selection of restaurants and shops is more limited compared to Bocagrande, so you might find yourself traveling to Bocagrande for more diverse dining or shopping options. Nonetheless, the tranquility and community spirit of El Laguito provide a nice balance to the more active areas of Cartagena.
