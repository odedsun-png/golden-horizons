# Colombia - Cost of Living in Cartagena’s Beach Districts — A Local's Perspective

Cartagena, with its population of over one million, is not just a tourist spot but a living city where retirees can find a reasonable cost of living. The average monthly expenses for a retiree in the beach districts can range from $1,500 to $2,500, which includes rent, utilities, groceries, dining out, and healthcare. This article will help you understand what living in Cartagena’s beach districts truly entails.

## What $2,000/Month Actually Buys in Bocagrande

With $2,000 a month, living in Bocagrande, one of the most popular beach districts in Cartagena, is quite achievable. Here, you can rent a modern one-bedroom apartment with ocean views for about $700 to $1,000 monthly, depending on the building's amenities and location. Bocagrande is known for its high-rise buildings and proximity to the beach, offering a lifestyle that combines urban convenience with seaside tranquility.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1606602842475-a5852a58b399?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxMXx8Q29sb21iaWF8ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc3ODEyMjU4OHww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Groceries for a couple run approximately $250 monthly, especially if you're buying local produce at the Mercado de Bazurto. Dining out is affordable, with local meals costing around $4 to $8. A dinner for two at a mid-range restaurant in the area might set you back about $30.

Transportation is another area where you’ll find savings. The public bus system is efficient, and a single ride costs less than a dollar. Taxis are also inexpensive, with most in-town rides costing around $2 to $3. However, while Bocagrande is safe, its popularity means it can be crowded, especially during peak travel seasons, which can affect your daily experience.

## Healthcare Options and Costs in Manga

Healthcare quality is a crucial consideration for retirees, and Cartagena doesn’t disappoint. In the Manga neighborhood, you’ll find several reputable clinics and hospitals, such as the Hospital Naval de Cartagena and Clinica San Jose de Torices. These facilities offer a range of services from primary care to specialized treatments.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1718119617938-2a3b376fb7d6?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8Q29sb21iaWF8ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc3ODEyMjI2OXww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Healthcare costs in Cartagena are significantly lower than in the U.S. For instance, a consultation with a general practitioner costs around $30, and specialist visits are approximately $50. Prescription medications are also affordable, with many common drugs available over the counter at local pharmacies.

Private health insurance is available and advisable, with plans starting at around $100 per month, depending on your age and health condition. The quality of care is generally good, but language can be a barrier as not all medical staff are fluent in English. It's beneficial to have a basic understanding of Spanish or a translator app handy.

## Cultural Life and Unexpected Costs in El Laguito

El Laguito is another neighborhood that offers a cultural scene with easy access to the beach. The area hosts various festivals and events throughout the year, showcasing music, dance, and art that are integral to Cartagena's identity. This neighborhood, while slightly less expensive than Bocagrande, has its own appeal, especially if you enjoy being immersed in local culture.

Renting a one-bedroom apartment in El Laguito costs between $600 and $900 monthly. The area is known for its relaxed pace and friendly locals, which makes it a welcoming environment for retirees. However, it’s important to budget for unexpected costs like home maintenance or air conditioning repairs due to the humid climate.

Utilities, including electricity and water, usually amount to around $100 to $150 per month, but can increase with frequent use of air conditioning. Additionally, while Cartagena is generally affordable, imported goods can be pricey. If you have specific brands or products you prefer from home, you might find yourself paying a premium.
