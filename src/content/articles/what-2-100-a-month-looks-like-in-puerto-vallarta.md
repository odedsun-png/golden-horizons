# What $2,100 a Month Looks Like Living in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico (Rent Levels and Daily Costs by Neighborhood)

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** A $2,100 monthly budget can work in Puerto Vallarta if you choose an outside-center one-bedroom at about $734 rather than a central apartment near $1,376. A food allowance of $180-$250, utilities and internet of about $92-$265, a monthly bus pass near $23, and a short private doctor visit around $33 leave an estimated $468-$811 after core monthly costs. The central beach areas change the picture quickly: rent alone can take roughly two-thirds of the budget.

## Where Your $2,100 Goes: Rent, Groceries, and the Ordinary Week

The waterfront may be what catches your eye. The rent is what decides whether you can stay relaxed once the novelty wears off.

A one-bedroom outside Puerto Vallarta’s city center averages about $734 a month, while a central-area one-bedroom averages about $1,376. That roughly $642 gap is not abstract. It can become your grocery money, utility cushion, bus pass, meals out, or reserve for healthcare.

Zona Romántica represents the pricier central choice in this comparison, with city-center pricing near $1,376. Versalles and Marina Vallarta are worth walking in person because they give you a useful comparison with the $734 outside-center benchmark, though the research does not provide separate rent averages for either area. Ask about furnishings, deposits, lease length, and whether utilities are included. Those details can make two similarly priced listings feel very different.

Food costs are easier to manage when most meals begin at home. A careful single shopper can use a planning allowance of $180-$250 a month, built from current staple prices: a dozen eggs costs about $3.35, a liter of milk about $1.68, tomatoes about $1.97 per kilogram, apples about $2.95 per kilogram, and bread about $1.73-$3.47 a loaf.

Then comes the coastal wildcard: cooling. Basic household utilities range from about $58-$231, with internet around $34 and a mobile plan averaging about $27. A breezy apartment and an air-conditioned apartment can produce very different monthly bills, especially when the weather encourages more time indoors.

Getting around can remain modest if buses suit your routine. A one-way local fare is about $0.64, and a monthly public-transport pass is about $23. Dining is where lifestyle quietly enters the budget: a cappuccino runs about $4.40, an inexpensive lunch about $12.20, and a basic dinner for two at a neighborhood pub about $27.18.

Puerto Vallarta can reward a modest budget—but most of that reward begins with treating the waterfront as a place to enjoy, not necessarily the premium you pay every month.
