# India - Long-Stay Visa Options for American Retirees

India offers a rich cultural tapestry and diverse experiences, making it an intriguing option for American retirees considering long-term stays. However, navigating the visa landscape is essential, as India does not offer a retirement-specific visa. Retirees must explore other available visa options, each with specific conditions and limitations.

## Retirement Snapshot

- **Visa Options**: Tourist Visa (up to 10 years), e-Tourist Visa (30 days, 1 year, 5 years), OCI for eligible persons of Indian origin
- **Maximum Stay**: 180 days per calendar year for tourist visas; no limit for OCI holders
- **Registration**: Required for visas exceeding 180 days validity; OCI holders are exempt
- **Safety Advisory**: Level 2 ("Exercise increased caution"); Level 4 ("Do not travel") for Jammu & Kashmir
- **Healthcare**: No verified information available
- **Cost of Living**: No verified information available
- **Best Fit**: Persons of Indian origin eligible for OCI
- **Biggest Warning**: No retirement-specific visa; safety concerns for women traveling alone

## Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

India does not offer a retirement-specific visa for U.S. citizens. Instead, retirees can consider:

- **Tourist Visa**: Valid for up to 10 years, allowing multiple entries with a maximum stay of 180 days per calendar year.
- **e-Tourist Visa**: Available in 30-day, 1-year, and 5-year durations, with similar stay restrictions.
- **OCI**: Provides lifelong multiple-entry privileges for eligible persons of Indian origin, with no stay limit per visit.

Safety advisories recommend exercising increased caution, especially for women traveling alone and avoiding certain regions like Jammu & Kashmir.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a retirement visa for India?**  
No, India does not offer a retirement-specific visa for U.S. citizens.

**What is the maximum stay allowed on a tourist visa?**  
The maximum stay is 180 days per calendar year for tourist visas.

**Do OCI holders need to register with local authorities?**  
No, OCI holders are exempt from registration requirements.
