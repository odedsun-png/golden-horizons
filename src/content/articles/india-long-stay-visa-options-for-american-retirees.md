# India - Long-Stay Visa Options for American Retirees

For American retirees considering an extended stay in India, understanding the visa options is essential. While India provides tourist visas with long validity, the actual time you can spend in the country each year is limited. This article outlines the available visa options and their implications for retirees.

## Retirement Snapshot

- **Visa Types**: Regular tourist visa (up to 10 years), e-Tourist visa (30 days, 1 year, 5 years)
- **Stay Limit**: Maximum of 180 days per calendar year
- **Registration**: Required with FRRO/FRO for stays over 180 days
- **Retirement Visa**: No specific category available
- **Entry Points**: e-Visas require entry through designated airports/seaports
- **Best Fit**: Retirees seeking extended visits rather than permanent relocation
- **Biggest Warning**: 180-day stay limit restricts continuous long-term residency

## Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

India offers a regular tourist visa valid for up to 10 years, but this does not equate to long-term residency. The stay is limited to 180 days per calendar year, requiring registration with the FRRO/FRO for any stay exceeding this duration. There is no specific retirement visa, so retirees must navigate the tourist visa framework. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I stay in India for more than 180 days in a year?**  
No, the maximum stay is 180 days per calendar year, regardless of visa validity.

**Is there a specific retirement visa for India?**  
No, India does not offer a retirement-specific visa for American retirees.

**What happens if I stay over 180 days?**  
You must register with the FRRO/FRO within 14 days of arrival if you plan to stay over 180 days.
