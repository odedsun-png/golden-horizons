## The Planning-Budget Reality

The Property Development Scheme (PDS) provides a legal framework for non-citizens to acquire residential property in Mauritius. While it offers a pathway to residency, it does not guarantee citizenship. The scheme is designed for luxury property acquisition, and retirees should verify current costs and regulatory changes, especially regarding payment structures and currency requirements.

## The Residency Reality

Non-citizens purchasing property under the PDS may apply for residence permits. The Economic Development Board (EDB) and the Department of Home Affairs manage these processes. It's crucial to verify the latest residency requirements and procedures, as these can change and affect eligibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the PDS?**  
A government-authorized framework enabling non-citizens to acquire residential property in Mauritius, overseen by the EDB; not a citizenship program.

**Can purchasing under PDS lead to Mauritian citizenship?**  
No; PDS is a residency/property pathway, not a citizenship path.

**Who can buy under PDS?**  
Natural persons (citizens or non-citizens) and various legal forms (companies, trusts, foundations) under the PDS regime.

**Are there recent regulatory changes retirees should know?**  
Yes; amendments around 2024–2025 include payment-method updates; verify the current regime with official sources.

**Where can I get official guidance on residency tied to PDS?**  
Department of Home Affairs and Economic Development Board; official portals provide the steps for residence permits linked to property schemes.
