## Cost of Living in Tarragona for a Retiree on $2,500/Month

Living in Tarragona on $2,500 a month is feasible with careful budgeting. Rent for a one-bedroom apartment varies by neighborhood. In the city center, expect to pay €1,100–€1,350 (~$1,210-1,470) for a furnished unit, while areas like Llarga offer options from €600–€850 (~$660-935) unfurnished. Groceries cost between €180–€420 (~$195-460) monthly, depending on your shopping habits and choice of supermarkets like Carrefour or local markets such as Mercado Central.

Utilities, including electricity, water, and internet, range from €100–€250 (~$110-275) per month. Public transportation is affordable, with monthly bus passes costing €40–€90 (~$44-99). Dining out can be a treat, with casual meals priced at €10–€15 (~$11-16) and mid-range dinners for two around €40–€60 (~$44-66).

## Housing, Neighborhoods, and 1-Bedroom Rent in Tarragona

Tarragona offers diverse neighborhoods, each with unique characteristics and rental prices. The Old Town is charming but pricier, with one-bedroom apartments ranging from €1,100–€1,350 (~$1,210-1,470) furnished. In contrast, the Camp de Tarragona area provides more budget-friendly options, with rents from €600–€850 (~$660-935) unfurnished.

Lease terms typically run for a year, but short-term furnished rentals are available at a premium. Consider proximity to amenities like hospitals and public transport when choosing a neighborhood. Hospital Joan XXIII and Hospital Sant Pau i Santa Tecla are centrally located, ensuring healthcare access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a US retiree live in Tarragona on $2,500/month?

Yes, a US retiree can live comfortably in Tarragona on $2,500/month. Rent for a one-bedroom ranges from €600–€1,350 (~$650-1,470), depending on the neighborhood. (Source: Wise)

### What residency visa would a US retiree pursue in Spain?

A US retiree would typically pursue the Non-Lucrative Visa, which requires proof of sufficient income. Check the Spanish consulate for the 2026 income threshold. (Source: Spanish consulate)

### What are healthcare options and costs for expats in Tarragona?

Expats can access public healthcare with a convenio especial, while private insurance costs vary. Expect to pay €50–€150 (~$55-165) monthly for private coverage. (Source: Catalan health authority)

### What are the safest neighborhoods for retirees in Tarragona?

Neighborhoods like Llarga and Camp de Tarragona are considered safe, with lower crime rates compared to the city center. (Source: local safety dataset)

### What are hospital options near Tarragona for retirees?

Hospital Joan XXIII and Hospital Sant Pau i Santa Tecla are major hospitals in Tarragona, both within a 15-minute drive from the city center. (Source: hospital websites)
