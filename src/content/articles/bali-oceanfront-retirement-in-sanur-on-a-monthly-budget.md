# Bali - Oceanfront Retirement in Sanur on a Monthly Budget — A Local's Perspective

In Sanur, a quaint coastal town in Bali, you can enjoy an oceanfront lifestyle for as little as $1,500 a month. This might sound surprising given Bali's reputation as a tourist hotspot, but Sanur offers a more laid-back vibe compared to its bustling neighbors, Kuta and Seminyak. The town is known for its long stretch of sandy beach and calm waters, perfect for those leisurely morning walks. This article will help you understand how to live comfortably in Sanur on a modest budget, covering housing, healthcare, and daily life.

## What $1,500/Month Actually Buys in Sanur

For around $700 per month, you can rent a fully furnished one-bedroom villa with a private garden and pool, located just a short walk from Sanur Beach. Jalan Danau Tamblingan, the main street in Sanur, is lined with shops, cafes, and markets, offering everything from fresh produce to local crafts. If you prefer a larger space, two-bedroom villas are available for about $1,200 per month. These often include amenities like air conditioning, Wi-Fi, and sometimes even housekeeping services.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/21544601/pexels-photo-21544601.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

Sanur offers a range of dining options to suit every budget. A meal at a local warung, a small family-owned restaurant, costs about $3, while a three-course meal at a mid-range restaurant will set you back around $15. The Sindhu Night Market is a popular spot for sampling local dishes at incredibly low prices. Here, you can enjoy a hearty meal of nasi goreng or satay for under $5.

While Sanur offers many affordable options, it's important to note that imported goods can be pricey due to taxes and shipping costs. Items like wine, cheese, and certain toiletries may cost significantly more than in the U.S. Therefore, adapting to local brands and products can help you stick to your budget.

## Navigating Healthcare in Sanur

Healthcare in Sanur is both accessible and affordable. The Bali Mandara Hospital, located on Jalan By Pass Ngurah Rai, is a well-equipped public hospital offering a range of services. For more specialized care, the BIMC Hospital in nearby Kuta provides high-quality healthcare services accredited by the Australian Council on Healthcare Standards.

A visit to a general practitioner in Sanur typically costs around $20, while a specialist consultation might range from $50 to $100. Most doctors speak English, and the medical staff are known for their professionalism and hospitality. Many pharmacies in Sanur offer a wide range of medications, often without the need for a prescription.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/30426383/pexels-photo-30426383.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

For those with ongoing medical needs, health insurance is crucial. International health insurance premiums vary but generally range from $100 to $300 per month, depending on your age and coverage level. It’s worth checking if your current insurance plan covers international healthcare or if you need to purchase a separate policy.

One limitation to consider is that while routine care is readily available, more serious medical issues might require travel to a larger city like Denpasar or even a flight to Singapore for advanced treatment. However, the local healthcare system is generally sufficient for most retirees' needs.

## Embracing Daily Life and Community in Sanur

Sanur is not just about the serene beaches; it's also about community and lifestyle. The Sanur Village Festival, held annually, is a vibrant celebration of Balinese culture, featuring traditional music, dance, and art. The festival is a great way to meet both locals and fellow expats, creating a sense of belonging in this tight-knit community.

For fitness enthusiasts, the beachfront promenade offers a perfect spot for jogging or cycling. The Sanur Cycle Tours provide guided bike tours through the town's scenic routes, allowing you to explore hidden gems and local neighborhoods. Yoga studios are abundant, with many offering classes tailored to older adults, ensuring a gentle yet effective practice.

Assumptions about Bali often include the notion of a completely relaxed pace of life, but Sanur offers a balance. While the town is generally peaceful, the local community is active and engaged. The Rotary Club of Bali Sanur and the Bali International Women's Association are just two of the organizations that welcome new members and offer various social activities.

A challenge you might face is the language barrier, as not everyone speaks English fluently. However, learning a few basic phrases in Bahasa Indonesia can go a long way in enhancing your experience and easing daily interactions. Local language classes are available and can be a fun way to immerse yourself in the culture.

In summary, Sanur offers an affordable, peaceful, and community-rich environment for retirees looking to enjoy an oceanfront lifestyle without breaking the bank. Whether it's the cost of living, healthcare options, or daily life, this article has painted a picture of what you can expect when you choose to retire in this beautiful part of Bali.
