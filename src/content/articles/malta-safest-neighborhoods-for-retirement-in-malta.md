# Malta - Safest Neighborhoods for Retirement in Malta — A Local's Perspective

With a population of just over 500,000, Malta offers a variety of Mediterranean lifestyle and English-speaking convenience, making it a popular choice for American retirees. The island nation is known for its historical sites, warm climate, and friendly locals. However, choosing the right neighborhood is crucial for ensuring a safe and comfortable retirement. This article will help you explore the safest neighborhoods in Malta and decide which might be the best fit for your retirement.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want a safe, English-speaking environment with a Mediterranean lifestyle.
- **Monthly reality:** Expect to spend around $2,500–$3,000, including rent, utilities, and groceries.
- **Biggest advantage:** High safety levels and a welcoming expat community.
- **Biggest warning:** Limited healthcare facilities on the island.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Sliema: A Popular Choice for Expats

Sliema, located along the northeastern coast of Malta, is a busy town with a population of approximately 17,000. Known for its modern amenities, Sliema offers a range of dining, shopping, and entertainment options. The area is particularly popular among expats due to its active community and English-speaking locals. Safety is a top priority here, with low crime rates and a visible police presence.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1717227268234-0a73de8479ff?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxMXx8TWFsdGF8ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc4MTk0NDgxOXww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

One of the main attractions in Sliema is the promenade, which stretches for several kilometers along the coast. It's a great spot for a morning walk or an evening stroll, offering impressive views of the Mediterranean Sea. The cost of living in Sliema can be higher compared to other parts of Malta. Rent for a one-bedroom apartment can range from €800 to €1,200 per month, depending on the location and amenities.

Despite its higher cost, Sliema's appeal lies in its convenience and safety. The town is well-connected by public transport, making it easy to explore other parts of the island. However, traffic congestion can be an issue, especially during peak hours. Retirees looking for a safe, lively environment with plenty of amenities will find Sliema an attractive option.

## St. Julian's: A Busy Yet Safe Neighborhood

St. Julian's, located just north of Sliema, is another popular choice for retirees seeking a safe and active environment. This town is renowned for its entertainment options, including restaurants, bars, and clubs. Despite its lively atmosphere, St. Julian's maintains a reputation for safety, with low crime rates and a strong sense of community.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1682107582866-e09ba8ee664f?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8TWFsdGF8ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc4MTk0NDgxOXww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

The neighborhood of Spinola Bay offers a picturesque setting with its colorful boats and waterfront restaurants. Here, retirees can enjoy leisurely meals while taking in the beautiful views of the bay. Housing in St. Julian's can be expensive, with one-bedroom apartments typically costing between €900 and €1,300 per month.

Public transportation is readily available, with buses connecting St. Julian's to other parts of Malta. However, the town's popularity means it can become crowded, especially during the tourist season. Retirees who enjoy a busy environment with plenty of dining and entertainment options will find St. Julian's a suitable choice. Be aware of the higher cost of living and the potential for noise in certain areas.

## Mellieha: A Peaceful Retreat with Safety in Mind

For those seeking a quieter, more relaxed environment, Mellieha offers a peaceful retreat on the northern coast of Malta. With a population of around 10,000, Mellieha is known for its impressive beaches and natural beauty. The area is considered one of the safest in Malta, with a strong sense of community and low crime rates.

Mellieha Bay, also known as Ghadira Bay, is the largest sandy beach in Malta and a popular spot for swimming and sunbathing. The town is also home to several nature reserves and hiking trails, making it an ideal location for retirees who enjoy outdoor activities. Housing in Mellieha is more affordable than in Sliema or St. Julian's, with one-bedroom apartments ranging from €600 to €900 per month.

While Mellieha offers a tranquil lifestyle, consider the limited public transportation options. Buses are available, but they may not run as frequently as in more urban areas. Retirees who prioritize safety and a slower pace of life will find Mellieha an appealing option, though they should be prepared for a quieter social scene.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

Healthcare in Malta is generally of good quality, but the island's limited size means that facilities can be stretched, especially during peak tourist seasons. Retirees should consider private health insurance to ensure access to necessary treatments. Verify healthcare access and insurance needs before moving.

Visa and residency rules for American retirees can be complex. Verify these requirements with official sources or a qualified professional to ensure compliance with Maltese law. Additionally, consider the tax implications of retiring in Malta, both in terms of U.S. and local taxes.

Safety is generally high across Malta, with low crime rates and a strong sense of community. However, it's always wise to check local safety conditions and walkability, especially if you plan to live in a more rural area.
