# Costa Rica - Best Cities for Retirees: Atenas vs Escazú — A Local's Perspective

A surprising fact about Costa Rica is that Atenas and Escazú, both popular among retirees, offer vastly different experiences despite being less than an hour's drive from each other. Atenas is often touted as having the best climate in the world, while Escazú, known as the "Beverly Hills of Costa Rica," offers a more urban experience. This article aims to help you decide which city might suit your retirement needs better.

## What $2,000/Month Actually Buys in Atenas

With a budget of $2,000 per month, life in Atenas can be both comfortable and fulfilling. Atenas, located about 25 miles west of San José, is celebrated for its mild climate. In this small town, $2,000 stretches further than you might think. Rent for a modest two-bedroom house can range from $500 to $800 a month, especially if you look in neighborhoods like Roca Verde or Barrio Mercedes. Utilities, including electricity and water, typically add another $100 to $150. Grocery shopping at local markets, such as the Atenas Farmers' Market, provides fresh produce at reasonable prices, often around $200 a month.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/36409629/pexels-photo-36409629.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

Dining out is both affordable and delightful. A meal at a local sodas, such as Soda La Fuente, will set you back about $5 to $7. If you enjoy dining at more upscale places, Restaurante La Trocha del Boyero offers meals for approximately $20. Transport is straightforward, with buses to San José costing less than $2, and local taxis available for short trips around town.

One limitation to note is the healthcare access in Atenas. While there is a local clinic, serious medical needs often require a trip to San José. For those with complex healthcare requirements, this is an important factor to consider. Nevertheless, Atenas offers a peaceful, community-focused lifestyle that many find rewarding.

## Escazú: Urban Amenities and Expat Community

In Escazú, $2,000 per month offers a different lifestyle, marked by urban comforts and a lively expat community. Located just a few miles from downtown San José, Escazú is known for its upscale amenities. Renting a one-bedroom apartment in a secure condominium complex such as Trejos Montealegre can start at around $1,000. This includes access to features like 24-hour security and swimming pools. Utility costs are similar to Atenas, but expect to spend a bit more on dining and entertainment.

For those who love shopping, Multiplaza Escazú offers a variety of international and local brands. Dining in Escazú ranges from affordable options like La Cebichería to high-end restaurants such as The Market at the InterContinental Hotel, where meals can range from $15 to $50. Healthcare is a strong point here, with top-notch facilities like Hospital CIMA offering comprehensive medical services, making it an attractive option for retirees prioritizing health access.

Public transport in Escazú is reliable, with frequent buses to San José and other areas. The local taxis and rideshare services like Uber are readily available, enhancing convenience.

One potential downside is the cost of living, which is higher than in Atenas. The expat community can make daily life feel a little less authentically Costa Rican, which might not appeal to everyone. However, for those who enjoy urban living with all its conveniences, Escazú presents an appealing option.

## Cultural Experiences and Lifestyle in Atenas and Escazú

Cultural experiences in Atenas are rooted in its local community and traditional Costa Rican lifestyle. The town hosts several annual festivals, including the Atenas Climate Fair, which celebrates the town's renowned weather. Local events at the Central Park often include music performances and artisanal markets. The slower pace of life here allows you to engage deeply with Costa Rican culture, from learning Spanish to participating in community initiatives. However, English is less commonly spoken, which could be challenging for some.

In contrast, Escazú offers a more cosmopolitan cultural scene. The city is home to a significant number of expats, which can be both a benefit and a drawback depending on your perspective. English is widely spoken, making the transition smoother for newcomers. Cultural activities are diverse, with art galleries, theaters, and international events frequently taking place. The Escazú festival, held annually, showcases both local and international artists and performers.

The lifestyle in Escazú is more fast-paced, with numerous opportunities for social and recreational activities. The city boasts several golf courses, sports clubs, and gyms. For a relaxing day, you might visit the Costa Rica Country Club or enjoy a walk through the Avenida Escazú, a popular shopping and dining district.
