# Costa Rica - Best Cities for Retirees: Atenas vs Escazú — A Local's Perspective

Atenas, a small town located about 27 miles from San José, is often touted as having "the best climate in the world" due to its moderate temperatures and low humidity. Meanwhile, Escazú, a suburb of San José, is known for its modern amenities and proximity to the capital. Both cities offer unique lifestyles for retirees, but they cater to different preferences and needs. This article will help you decide which of these two Costa Rican cities might be the best fit for your retirement.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want either a small-town feel with great weather (Atenas) or urban convenience with modern amenities (Escazú).
- **Monthly reality:** Atenas can be more budget-friendly with costs around $1,500–$2,000, while Escazú might require $2,500–$3,500 for a similar lifestyle.
- **Biggest advantage:** Atenas offers a peaceful environment with a strong expat community, while Escazú provides easy access to high-quality healthcare and shopping.
- **Biggest warning:** Atenas may lack some modern conveniences, while Escazú can be significantly more expensive.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Life in Atenas: Climate, Community, and Costs

Atenas is approximately 27 miles west of San José, offering a small-town atmosphere with a population of about 27,000. The town is known for its pleasant climate, with average temperatures ranging from 70 to 80 degrees Fahrenheit year-round. This makes it an appealing choice for retirees looking to escape extreme weather conditions.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1550853123-b81beb0b1449?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxMXx8Q29zdGElMjBSaWNhfGVufDB8MHx8fDE3NzgxODIzNzd8MA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

The local community in Atenas is welcoming, with a significant number of American and Canadian expats. The town offers a variety of social clubs and activities, making it easy to integrate and meet new people. Atenas is home to several local markets, such as the Atenas Farmers Market, where fresh produce and local goods are available weekly.

While Atenas offers a laid-back lifestyle, it does have limitations. The town lacks some modern conveniences found in larger cities, such as major shopping centers and a wide range of dining options. Healthcare facilities are basic, and for more specialized care, residents often travel to San José.

## Escazú: Modern Amenities and Urban Convenience

Escazú, located just 6 miles from San José, is known for its upscale living and modern amenities. With a population of about 60,000, this suburb offers a more urban lifestyle compared to Atenas. The area is home to several shopping malls, such as Multiplaza Escazú, which features international brands and a variety of dining options.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579380231498-e45d45213373?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8Q29zdGElMjBSaWNhfGVufDB8MHx8fDE3NzgxODIzNzd8MA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Healthcare in Escazú is excellent, with access to top-notch facilities like Hospital CIMA, a private hospital that offers a wide range of services and specialists. This makes Escazú an attractive option for retirees who prioritize healthcare access.

However, living in Escazú comes with a higher price tag. Housing costs can be significantly higher than in Atenas, with monthly rents ranging from $1,200 to $2,500 depending on the neighborhood and property type. Additionally, the cost of living, including dining and shopping, tends to be more expensive.

## Comparing Community and Lifestyle

While both Atenas and Escazú have active expat communities, the lifestyle in each city differs significantly. Atenas offers a more rural, relaxed environment, ideal for those seeking a slower pace of life. The town is surrounded by lush greenery and offers opportunities for outdoor activities like hiking and bird watching.

In contrast, Escazú caters to those who prefer urban living with easy access to modern conveniences. The area is more developed, with a range of restaurants, cafes, and cultural events. The proximity to San José also means that residents can enjoy the amenities of the capital city without the need to live in its activity level.

One common assumption is that Costa Rica is always affordable, but this isn't always the case. While Atenas can be budget-friendly, Escazú's cost of living can rival that of some American cities, especially when factoring in housing and healthcare. Retirees should assess their lifestyle preferences and financial situation when choosing between these two locations.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

When planning a move to Costa Rica, healthcare access is a vital consideration. While both Atenas and Escazú have healthcare facilities, Escazú offers more comprehensive services with private hospitals like Hospital CIMA. Retirees should evaluate their healthcare needs and consider private insurance options.

Visa and residency rules in Costa Rica require careful attention. Most retirees opt for the Pensionado visa, which requires proof of a stable monthly income of at least $1,000. Consulting with an immigration expert to understand the latest requirements and ensure compliance is recommended.

Tax obligations can be complex, as retirees must consider both U.S. and Costa Rican tax laws. Consulting with a tax professional familiar with international tax issues is advisable to avoid unexpected liabilities.

Safety is generally good in both Atenas and Escazú, but it's always wise to stay informed about local crime trends and take standard precautions to protect personal property.

## Common Mistakes Retirees Should Avoid

- Overlooking the cost of living differences between Atenas and Escazú.
- Assuming healthcare access is the same in both locations.
- Not verifying residency and visa requirements before moving.
- Underestimating the importance of learning some Spanish for daily interactions.
- Ignoring the need for private health insurance.
- Failing to consider transportation needs and options.
- Forgetting to factor in potential tax obligations in both the U.S. and Costa Rica.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is healthcare in Atenas adequate for retirees?

Atenas offers basic healthcare facilities, but for specialized care or emergencies, residents often travel to San José. It's advisable to have private insurance for access to better facilities.

### How do the costs in Atenas compare to Escazú?

Atenas is generally more affordable, with monthly expenses ranging from $1,500 to $2,000. Escazú, being more urban and upscale, can cost between $2,500 and $3,500 monthly.

### What are the transportation options in these cities?

Atenas has limited public transport, so owning a car may be necessary. Escazú offers better public transport options and is closer to San José, making travel more convenient.
