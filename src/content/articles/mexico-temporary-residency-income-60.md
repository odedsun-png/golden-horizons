# Mexico Temporary Residency for Retirees: Income, Savings, and What to Bring

**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING — VERIFY RESIDENCY

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** Orlando's temporary resident checklist lists no minimum age, and you can qualify through pension income or savings. Orlando asks for at least $4,500 a month after tax for 6 months, or a $74,500 average balance over 12 months. Boise asks for more than $4,400 a month, or a $75,000 average balance. The catch: each consulate sets its own checklist, so apply where your address says you must, and you have 30 days after entry to apply for your resident card.

## Retirement Snapshot

**Primary decision:** Whether to apply for a temporary resident visa on pension income or on savings, and at which consulate.  
**Best fit:** Americans with steady pension or Social Security deposits, or a bank balance that has stayed high for a full year.  
**Biggest warning:** Two consulates publish two different sets of numbers, and a recent transfer will not lift a 12-month average.  
**Residency:** The visa is single-entry, and the consulate fee is $56 whether or not you are approved.  
**Healthcare:** Medicare pays $0 for routine care in Mexico, so budget separate cover for a clinic such as Hospital Angeles Tijuana or Hospital CIMA.  
**Safety:** The State Department rates Mexico Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, in its advisory dated May 28, 2026. Check the state you plan to live in, since some states carry higher levels.

## Orlando and Boise: Two Consulates, Two Sets of Numbers

Both consulates publish a financial test for the temporary resident visa. The figures below come from their own checklists.

| Consulate | Income option | Savings option |
|---|---|---|
| Orlando | At least $4,500 a month after tax, for the previous 6 months | Average monthly balance of $74,500 over the previous 12 months |
| Boise | More than $4,400 a month net, for the last 6 months | Average balance of $75,000 over the previous 12 months |

Boise also lists a Mexican property deed worth more than $597,694.45 and adds $1,400 to the solvency test for each dependent. Orlando lists family unity as a separate route.

Boise's income bar is lower and its savings bar is higher. Read the checklist of the office that serves your address, and do not shop for the friendliest number. Neither list shows what Mexico costs to live in. Numbeo's country page, updated November 21, 2026 from 794 contributors, puts a single person's monthly costs at about $655 before rent, so the visa bar is a paperwork test and not a budget.

## Pension Income or Savings: Which Records Fit You

### If you use pension income

Count the last 6 months of deposits. Orlando asks for income after tax, so check whether your pension letter shows gross or net amounts. If you add Social Security to a pension, make sure each deposit shows on a statement the bank will validate.

Orlando's employer letter applies to employment income and remote work. Pension applicants do not need one.

### If you use savings

Both offices average the balance over 12 months. A large deposit last month will not lift a 12-month average to the bar. If your money sits in more than one bank, ask the consulate how to document the accounts before you pay for letters.

## What to Bring and What Validation Means

Orlando's basic file is a valid passport and photocopy, a completed application form with a photo, and proof of lawful status if you apply from a country that is not your own. Add the financial records for your route.

Statements must be stamped by the bank, come with a bank letter validating them, or be an electronic document with a digital certificate. A plain PDF from online banking may not pass, so show your bank the checklist when you ask.

Orlando says processing takes 1 to 10 business days and the decision is final. Book your appointment at citas.sre.gob.mx only when your file is complete.

## After You Arrive: The 30-Day Card Deadline

The visa is single-entry and an immigration officer decides at the port of entry. Once inside, you must apply for the Temporary Resident Card within 30 calendar days. Boise says the exchange takes place at an INM office and takes 2 to 4 weeks. It lists validity of 1 to 4 years, depending on what INM authorizes.

Find the INM office near your first address before you fly. Mexico's one-stop procedure page repeats the 30-day rule for exchanging the visa.

## Practical Comparison

### Pension route

**What the evidence says:**  
Orlando requires at least $4,500 a month after tax for 6 months, and Boise requires more than $4,400 net for 6 months.

**What to check next:**  
Ask your consulate whether Social Security plus a pension can be added together, and whether it wants gross or net figures on the statements.

### Savings route

**What the evidence says:**  
Orlando requires a $74,500 average balance and Boise requires $75,000, both averaged over 12 months.

**What to check next:**  
Ask your bank for a 12-month average balance letter and confirm the monthly averages sit above the bar.

### Validation of statements

**What the evidence says:**  
Orlando accepts a bank stamp, a validation letter, or an electronic document with a digital certificate.

**What to check next:**  
Send your bank the checklist and ask which of the 3 it can issue and how many days it takes.

### Health cover after the move

**What the evidence says:**  
Medicare's fact sheet says it pays for care outside the U.S. only in 3 narrow hospital situations, and $0 for drugs bought abroad.

**What to check next:**  
Get 2 quotes for private cover for residents, and ask whether pre-existing conditions are excluded.

## The Trade-Off

Temporary residency lets you test Mexico with legal status for 1 to 4 years. It also locks your application to one consulate's paperwork rules, and a missed 30-day deadline can cost you the card.

**What you may gain:**
- A legal base for a long stay, with a card that proves status after you enter.
- A choice between a 6-month income test and a 12-month savings test.

**What you may give up:**
- A non-refundable $56 fee even if the consulate says no.
- Months of record-gathering, since validated statements take days to arrange.

## The Golden Horizons 30-Day Test

1. Find the consulate that serves your address and download its 2026 temporary residence checklist.
2. Add up your last 6 months of net pension and Social Security deposits and compare the total to your consulate's monthly bar.
3. Ask your bank for a 12-month average balance report and the validation letter or stamp.
4. Call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) and ask what Medicare covers in Mexico, then get 2 quotes for resident health insurance.
5. Book a visit to Mexico of 2 to 4 weeks and visit the INM office near the town where you would live.
6. Do not book the consular appointment until every document on the checklist is in your folder.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much income do I need for Mexico temporary residency?

Orlando asks for at least $4,500 a month after tax for the previous 6 months. Boise asks for more than $4,400 a month net, so confirm the figure with your own consulate. ([consulmex.sre.gob.mx](https://consulmex.sre.gob.mx/orlando/images/pdfs/2026/TEMPORARY_RESIDENCE_english.pdf))

### How long do I have to apply for the resident card?

You have 30 calendar days after entering Mexico to exchange the visa for the resident card. ([ventanillaunica.economia.gob.mx](https://ventanillaunica.economia.gob.mx/procedure/296/step/604?l=en))

### Is Mexico safe for retirees right now?

The State Department rates Mexico Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, in an advisory dated May 28, 2026. It advises following U.S. government employee limits such as no travel between cities after dark. ([cadataapi.state.gov](https://cadataapi.state.gov/api/TravelAdvisories))

### Will Medicare cover me once I live in Mexico?

No, Medicare pays for a foreign hospital in only 3 narrow situations and pays $0 for drugs bought outside the U.S. Part B costs $202.90 a month in 2026, so decide whether to keep it. ([medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/publications/11037-medicare-coverage-outside-the-united-states.pdf))

### What does daily life cost in Mexico?

Numbeo's country page puts a single person's monthly costs at about $655 before rent, based on 794 contributors. Rent varies by city, so check the city page. ([numbeo.com](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/country_result.jsp?country=Mexico))
