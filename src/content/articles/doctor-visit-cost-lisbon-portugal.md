## Healthcare Costs in Lisbon: What a Doctor Visit Actually Costs

In Lisbon, healthcare costs vary significantly between the public and private sectors. A private general practitioner (GP) visit typically costs between €40 and €90, depending on the clinic and whether it is an initial or follow-up consultation. For emergency or urgent care in private facilities, initial evaluations can range from €100 to €180, with additional charges for tests or procedures.

The public healthcare system, known as SNS, offers more affordable options, especially for residents. Fees in the SNS are generally lower and can be subsidized, but they vary based on the service type and the patient's residency status. Enrollment in the SNS is possible for foreigners, which can significantly reduce healthcare costs.

## Public vs Private Pathways: How the Portuguese System Works for Foreign Retirees

Portugal's healthcare system is divided into public and private sectors. The SNS provides comprehensive coverage at lower costs, but it often involves longer wait times. Foreign retirees can access the SNS through specific enrollment regimes, which require understanding the eligibility criteria and necessary documentation.

Private healthcare in Lisbon offers quicker access to services, but at a higher cost. Many retirees opt for private insurance to manage these expenses, ensuring they have access to both routine and specialist care without long waits. It's crucial for retirees to evaluate their healthcare needs and budget accordingly, considering both public and private options.
