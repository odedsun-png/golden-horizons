## Hidden Colonial Towns Reality

### Sucre (Chuquisaca)

Sucre, the constitutional capital of Bolivia, is renowned for its colonial architecture and whitewashed streets. It is often highlighted as a budget-friendly destination for expats and retirees. The city's cultural heritage and accessible expat community make it a favorable choice for those seeking a colonial atmosphere.

### Betanzos (Potosí Department)

Betanzos, located near Potosí, offers a small-town feel with colonial-era architecture and surrounding haciendas. While it has limited expat infrastructure, its heritage charm is appealing for those interested in a quieter lifestyle.

### Torotoro (Potosí Department)

Known for its natural and archaeological sites, Torotoro provides a unique blend of colonial roots and outdoor attractions. It is less developed in terms of expat services, making it ideal for adventurous retirees.
