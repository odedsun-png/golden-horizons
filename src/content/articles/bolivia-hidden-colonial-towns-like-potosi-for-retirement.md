## Colonial Towns Reality

### Sucre

Sucre, Bolivia's constitutional capital, is renowned for its colonial architecture and vibrant expat community. It is often highlighted in expat guides as an affordable city for retirees. The climate is mild, and the city offers a range of amenities that cater to a modest-budget lifestyle. However, potential retirees should verify residency requirements, as Bolivia does not offer a specific retirement visa.
