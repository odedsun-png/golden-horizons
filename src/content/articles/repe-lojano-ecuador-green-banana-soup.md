## Quick Taste

As the afternoon sun filters through the market stalls of Loja, Ecuador, you find yourself drawn to a small eatery where the aroma of repe lojano—a creamy green-banana soup—fills the air. This dish, with its smooth texture and subtle sweetness, is a staple in the southern Andean region. Made from green bananas, milk, and cheese, it’s a comforting bowl that’s both nourishing and satisfying. Typically served with a side of avocado and a sprinkle of cilantro, a bowl of repe lojano costs around $3 to $5, making it an affordable taste of everyday life in Ecuador.

## The Story Behind the Dish

Repe lojano has its roots in the Loja province of Ecuador, a region known for its agricultural traditions. The soup reflects the area's reliance on locally grown produce, particularly the green banana, which thrives in the Andean climate. Historically, this dish was a way to make use of the abundant bananas that were not yet ripe. Over time, it evolved into a staple, celebrated for its simplicity and heartiness. The addition of milk and cheese gives the soup its characteristic creaminess, while the green bananas provide a unique flavor that sets it apart from other Ecuadorian soups.

## How Locals Eat It

In Loja, repe lojano is often enjoyed as a midday meal, providing a warm respite from the cooler mountain air. Locals typically pair it with a slice of fresh bread or a handful of toasted corn kernels for added texture. It's common to see families gathering around the table, sharing stories and laughter over steaming bowls of this comforting soup. The avocado served alongside is not just a garnish but an integral part of the meal, adding richness and a contrasting creaminess that complements the soup's flavors. For many Lojanos, repe lojano is more than just food; it’s a connection to their heritage and community.

## Regional Variations

Though repe lojano is primarily associated with Loja, variations of the soup can be found throughout the Andean region. In some areas, cooks add a touch of garlic or onion to enhance the flavor, while others might include a handful of fresh herbs like cilantro or parsley. In the neighboring province of Zamora-Chinchipe, you might encounter a version that incorporates local spices, adding a subtle heat to the dish. Despite these differences, the core ingredients—green bananas, milk, and cheese—remain the same, ensuring that the essence of repe lojano is preserved across the region.

## Cost, Portions, and Ordering Tips

When ordering repe lojano in a local eatery, expect to pay between $3 and $5 for a generous portion that is often enough to satisfy a hearty appetite. If you're dining with friends, consider ordering a few side dishes to share, such as empanadas or fried plantains, which complement the soup's flavors beautifully. If you're making it at home, green bananas and other ingredients are readily available at local markets, with a typical shopping trip costing around $10 for all the necessary components. Remember, the key to a great repe lojano is using fresh, high-quality ingredients.

## What to Know Before You Try It

Repe lojano is a dish that embodies the spirit of the Andean highlands, offering a taste of Ecuadorian culture in every spoonful. Additionally, some travelers miss out on the full experience by not trying the soup in a local market setting, where the atmosphere and freshness of the ingredients enhance the meal. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the main ingredient in repe lojano?

The primary ingredient in repe lojano is green bananas, which are used for their starchy texture and mild flavor. These bananas are essential to achieving the soup's creamy consistency.

### Is repe lojano vegetarian?

Yes, repe lojano is typically vegetarian, as it is made with green bananas, milk, cheese, and sometimes herbs and spices. However, always check with the restaurant to ensure no meat-based broths are used.

### Can I find repe lojano outside of Loja?

While repe lojano is most commonly associated with Loja, it can be found in traditional Andean restaurants throughout Ecuador. It's less common in coastal areas, where seafood dishes dominate.

### How does repe lojano differ from other Ecuadorian soups?

Repe lojano is distinct due to its use of green bananas, which give it a unique texture and flavor. Unlike many Ecuadorian soups that feature meat or seafood, repe lojano is primarily vegetarian and dairy-based.
