## Understanding the 90-Day Visa-Free Rule

Montenegro offers US citizens visa-free entry for up to 90 days within a 180-day period. This rule allows for short-term stays without the need for a visa, making it an attractive option for retirees exploring the country. The 90-day limit is calculated within a rolling 180-day window, meaning that once you have spent 90 days in Montenegro, you must leave and wait for the remaining days of the 180-day period before re-entering.

### Re-entry Considerations

If you plan to return to Montenegro after your initial 90-day stay, you must ensure that you comply with the 180-day counting window. This means calculating your days carefully to avoid overstaying, which could lead to penalties or restrictions on future entries.

## Extending Your Stay Beyond 90 Days

For those wishing to stay longer than 90 days, Montenegro offers long-stay visa options. The D visa is a popular choice, allowing stays of up to 6 months within a 12-month period. This visa requires a formal application process and adherence to Montenegrin immigration laws.

### Applying for a Long-Stay Visa

To apply for a D visa, you will need to submit an application to the Montenegrin authorities, providing necessary documentation such as proof of accommodation and financial means. It's essential to start this process well before your initial 90-day period ends to ensure a seamless transition.
