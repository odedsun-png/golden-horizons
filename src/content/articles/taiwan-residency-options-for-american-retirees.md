## The Residency Reality

Taiwan does not provide a straightforward residency route for retirees based solely on passive income or Social Security. The available residency options are primarily through employment, investment, or family ties, each with significant requirements:

- **Employment:** The Employment Gold Card offers a path to residency for qualified professionals, but this is not a retirement route.  
- **Investment:** Permanent residency requires a minimum investment of NTD 15 million in a business creating jobs or NTD 30 million in government bonds for at least three years, a high financial hurdle.  
- **Family Ties:** Marriage-based residency is available but requires marriage to a Taiwanese citizen, not applicable to most baseline retirees.

The Alien Permanent Resident Certificate (APRC) requires five years of residence with an Alien Resident Certificate (ARC), proof of self-support/assets or skills, and no criminal record. Eligibility for retirees on passive income alone is unresolved and likely requires additional conditions such as employment or significant assets.
