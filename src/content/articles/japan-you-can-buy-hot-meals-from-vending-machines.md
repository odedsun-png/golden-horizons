# Hot meals available from vending machines in Japan

> **Did you know?** In Japan, vending machines can serve you a hot bowl of ramen!

Japan is famous for its innovative vending machines, and some of the most intriguing ones offer hot meals. Imagine walking up to a vending machine and getting a steaming bowl of ramen or even a hot hamburger. These machines, like the RAMEN STOCK 24, are not just about convenience—they're a culinary experience.

While many vending machines in Japan dispense drinks, a select few provide hot, ready-to-eat meals. These machines can be found in specialized locations, such as a vending-machine cafeteria in Gunma. They offer a quick and unattended dining option, perfect for those on the go.

Next time you're in Japan, keep an eye out for these unique vending machines. They might just surprise you with a delicious, hot meal when you least expect it!
