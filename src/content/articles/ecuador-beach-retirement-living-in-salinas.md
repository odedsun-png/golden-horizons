# Ecuador - Beach Retirement Living in Salinas — A Local's Perspective

Ecuador beach town Salinas offers retirees a perfect blend of affordable oceanside living with the comforts of a small city. Discover how this coastal retreat provides an inviting setting for American retirees seeking a sunny, budget-friendly retirement destination.

## Lifestyle and Amenities in Salinas

Retiring in Salinas means enjoying a range of amenities including local markets, health care, and recreational activities. The town's infrastructure supports a laid-back yet comfortable lifestyle, providing everything from beachfront bars to golf courses.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/32380332/pexels-photo-32380332.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

Moreover, the community is welcoming, with many expatriates choosing Salinas for its tight-knit social scene.

## Cost of Living Advantages

A significant advantage of living in Salinas is the low cost of living. Housing, healthcare, and daily expenses are remarkably affordable compared to the U.S., with beachfront condos starting below $100,000.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/34911602/pexels-photo-34911602.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

This affordability, combined with a readily accessible lifestyle, makes Salinas an attractive option for retirees on a fixed income.
