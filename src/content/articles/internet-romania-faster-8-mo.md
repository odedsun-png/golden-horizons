## Romania: A Fast, Affordable Internet Option for Retirees

Romania's internet is not just fast; it's also incredibly affordable. With broadband plans typically ranging from €6 to €8 per month (~$6.50–$8.50), it's one of the cheapest in the EU. In contrast, U.S. broadband prices are often much higher, making Romania an attractive option for retirees who rely on digital connectivity.

The speed is another highlight. Romania's internet speeds have been on the rise, with major cities like Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca offering high-speed connections that rival or exceed those in the U.S. This makes it ideal for streaming, video calls, and other bandwidth-intensive activities.

## What a Retiree's Typical Monthly Internet-Enabled Life Could Look Like in Romania

Living in Romania with such affordable internet opens up a world of possibilities. Imagine renting a one-bedroom apartment in Bucharest for €300–€420/month (~$325–$455) unfurnished, or €420–€650/month (~$455–$705) furnished. Groceries might set you back €150–€230/month (~$163–$250), while utilities, including internet, could cost €46–€78/month (~$50–$85).

Dining out is also affordable, with monthly costs ranging from €100 to €180 (~$108–$195). Public transportation is efficient and cheap, costing around €25–€40/month (~$27–$43). This setup allows retirees to enjoy a comfortable lifestyle without breaking the bank.
