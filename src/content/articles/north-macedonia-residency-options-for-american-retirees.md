## Why This Is Worth Testing

Retiring in North Macedonia presents an intriguing opportunity for those willing to navigate its residency requirements. While the country does not offer a straightforward retirement visa, it could still be worth considering for those with the means to invest in property or with existing family ties in the region. The cost of living in North Macedonia is generally lower than in many U.S. cities, potentially allowing your $2,500 monthly budget to stretch further. However, retirees must be prepared for the complexities of securing a temporary residence permit, which requires a specific legal purpose such as property ownership, work, or family reunification.

The absence of a direct retiree-specific residence permit means that retirees must rely on other qualifying grounds. Temporary residence permits are renewable, but they require active compliance with local laws and regulations. Permanent residence is an option after five years of continuous legal residence, but this path demands a long-term commitment and adherence to additional conditions such as language proficiency and stable income.

## The Residency Reality

North Macedonia does not offer a retirement-specific residence permit for American retirees. Instead, temporary residence permits are available under the Law on Foreigners for specified purposes such as work, study, family reunification, or property ownership. These permits are typically valid for up to one year and are renewable if the conditions continue to be met. However, they require a specific legal purpose beyond passive retirement income, making it challenging for retirees without local employment, family ties, or investment to qualify.

Permanent residence is possible after five years of continuous legal residence under qualifying permits. This path includes conditions such as language proficiency, stable income, housing, and potentially renouncing prior citizenship. Retirees interested in this route should be prepared for a long-term commitment and verify the current requirements with official sources.
