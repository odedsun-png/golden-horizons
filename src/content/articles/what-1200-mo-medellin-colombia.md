Dining out at a local restaurant costs about COP 20,000 (~$5) per meal. Healthcare access is available through private insurance, with costs varying by provider.

## Cost Snapshot for Medellín at $1,200/month

Medellín offers a range of living costs that can fit a $1,200 monthly budget, but location matters. In neighborhoods like Laureles and Envigado, a furnished one-bedroom apartment rents for COP 2.1–3.0 million (~$550-800). In El Poblado, expect higher rents. Groceries for one person typically cost around COP 2.3 million (~$600) monthly.

Utilities, including electricity, water, and internet, average COP 300,000 (~$80). Public transport is efficient and affordable, with metro fares at COP 2,550 (~$0.65) per ride. Dining out at a local restaurant costs about COP 20,000 (~$5) per meal, while a coffee at a café is around COP 4,000 (~$1).

## Residency Pathways for Retirees in Medellín

Retirees can explore the pensionado visa, which requires proof of a stable pension income. While exact income thresholds need confirmation from Colombian authorities, this visa is a popular choice for expats. Processing times and documentation requirements vary, so consulting with an immigration attorney is advisable.

## Climate and Everyday Life in Medellín for Seniors

Medellín's climate is often called "eternal spring," with temperatures ranging from 64°F to 82°F year-round. This makes it ideal for outdoor activities and exploring neighborhoods like Laureles and Belén, known for their walkability and local markets. The city's public transit system, including the metro and cable cars, offers convenient access to various parts of the city without needing a car.
