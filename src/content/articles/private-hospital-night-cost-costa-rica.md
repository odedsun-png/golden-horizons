# What a Night in a Private Hospital Costs in Costa Rica
**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING — VERIFY COSTS

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** Costa Rica's private hospitals do not publish nightly room rates, so no verified price per night exists. For scale, U.S. community hospitals spent about $3,297 per adjusted inpatient day in 2024, which is the hospital's cost, not a patient bill. Original Medicare's 2026 Part A deductible is $1,736 per benefit period at home, but Medicare usually does not pay for care in Costa Rica. The catch is that you need a written quote before you are admitted, and Costa Rica is Level 2, "Exercise increased caution," as of April 1, 2026.

If you are 65 or older, the first question is who pays. Medicare usually does not cover care outside the United States, so a night in a Costa Rican private hospital is a cash or private-insurance cost.

Online guides quote nightly ranges, but none of the hospital sites we checked today list a room rate. This guide shows what could be confirmed, and what to ask for.

**Healthcare:**  
Clínica Bíblica says it is accredited by Joint Commission International and has locations in San José, Santa Ana, Aleste and Liberia. Its site lists no room prices and sends you to an online quote form or its call center.

**Housing / daily life:**  
Numbeo lists a single person's monthly costs in Costa Rica at $964.4 excluding rent, based on 104 contributors (updated November 12, 2026). It lists no hospital-room price.

**Safety:**  
The State Department rates Costa Rica Level 2, "Exercise increased caution," because of crime (April 1, 2026). Petty crime is common, and violent crime also affects tourists.

## What a Hospital Night Costs at Home

Original Medicare covers inpatient hospital care in the United States. In 2026 the Part A deductible is $1,736 for each benefit period. Days 61 to 90 carry coinsurance of $434 per day. Part B has a $283 annual deductible for doctor services.

With Medicare and a Medigap plan, a covered U.S. stay can cost you little out of pocket. That means home may be cheaper than a cash stay abroad, depending on your plan.

For scale on the hospital side, KFF reports that U.S. community hospitals spent $3,297.26 per adjusted inpatient day in 2024. KFF notes this is an estimate of expenses, not charges or reimbursement. It also includes outpatient volume. Do not read it as a price per night.

## What Costa Rica's Hospitals Publish

Clínica Bíblica's site describes hospital rooms with a private bathroom, closet, companion sofa bed, Wi-Fi and TV. Its Short Stay Unit rooms are shared, and its Critical Care Unit has eight individual cubicles.

The page lists no price. To get an estimate, you fill out an online form for surgeries and procedures, diagnostic imaging, pharmacy or maternity, and staff contact you. You can also call +506 2522-1000 or message +506 8595-7000 on WhatsApp.

Numbeo, a crowd-sourced price site, had 1,593 Costa Rica entries from 104 contributors in the past 12 months. It does not list a hospital-room price, so there is no neutral nightly figure to quote.

## What to Ask For in Writing

Ask each hospital to price these lines separately:

- The room, and what nursing care comes with it.
- Doctor and specialist fees.
- Tests, imaging and medications.
- Any procedure you may need.
- Deposit, payment method and insurance pre-authorization.
- What changes if the stay runs longer or you move to intensive care.

## Practical Comparison

### Costa Rica hospital night versus a U.S. hospital night

**What the evidence says:**  
No Costa Rican hospital checked today publishes a nightly rate. KFF's $3,297 U.S. figure is a hospital expense, not a bill, so the two numbers cannot be compared directly.

**What to check next:**  
Ask Clínica Bíblica and Hospital CIMA for a written room quote, then ask your U.S. insurer what the same night would cost you.

### Cash price versus Original Medicare

**What the evidence says:**  
Medicare usually does not cover health care outside the U.S., with narrow exceptions. Medigap policies may cover emergency care when you travel.

**What to check next:**  
Read your Medigap policy for foreign-travel emergency terms and limits, and ask whether any Costa Rica stay is excluded.

### Public care versus private care

**What the evidence says:**  
Private hospitals bill the patient or the insurer. Clínica Bíblica lists its quote channels but no prices.

**What to check next:**  
Ask each hospital whether a deposit is required on admission and which insurers it bills directly.

## The Trade-Off

Private care gives you a named hospital to call and an international accreditation to check. But you cannot price a night until the hospital sends a quote.

**What you may gain:**

- Access to an accredited private hospital such as Clínica Bíblica.
- A written quote you can compare against your U.S. out-of-pocket cost.
- A clear advisory level to check before you book: Level 2.

**What you may give up:**

- Medicare coverage, which usually stops at the U.S. border.
- A published price to plan around.
- Certainty about deposits and what the nightly rate includes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is a night in a private hospital in Costa Rica?

No published rate was found: Clínica Bíblica's hospitalization page lists no price and points to a quote form and a call center at +506 2522-1000. Ask for a written estimate before admission. ([clinicabiblica.com](https://www.clinicabiblica.com/en/services/general-services/hospitalization))

### What is the Medicare deductible for a U.S. hospital stay in 2026?

The Part A inpatient deductible is $1,736 per benefit period in 2026, and the Part B deductible is $283. Both apply to care in the United States. ([cms.gov](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2026-medicare-parts-b-premiums-deductibles))

### Does Medicare pay for a hospital stay in Costa Rica?

Usually not. Medicare says it "usually doesn't cover health care while you're traveling outside the U.S." with a few exceptions, and Medigap policies may cover emergency care abroad. Ask your Medigap insurer for its foreign-travel limits before you travel in 2026. ([medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/travel-outside-the-u.s.))

### How much does a U.S. hospital day cost?

KFF reports that U.S. community hospitals spent $3,297.26 per adjusted inpatient day in 2024. That is the hospital's expense, not what a patient is billed. ([kff.org](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/state-indicator/expenses-per-inpatient-day/))

### Is Costa Rica safe for older travelers?

The State Department rates Costa Rica Level 2, "Exercise increased caution," as of April 1, 2026, citing crime. Petty crime is common and violent crime also affects tourists.
