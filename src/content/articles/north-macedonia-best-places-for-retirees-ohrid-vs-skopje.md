# North Macedonia - Best Places for Retirees: Ohrid vs Skopje — A Local's Perspective

Ohrid, with its population of around 42,000, is a small city famous for its impressive lake and historical significance. Skopje, the capital, is home to over 500,000 residents and offers a busy urban environment combining old and new. This article will help you decide which city might suit your retirement plans by comparing costs, lifestyle, healthcare, and other practical considerations.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want either a lakeside retreat or a busy urban experience.
- **Monthly reality:** Expect to spend around $1,200 in Ohrid and $1,500 in Skopje for comfortable living.
- **Biggest advantage:** Ohrid offers tranquility and natural beauty, while Skopje provides modern amenities and cultural events.
- **Biggest warning:** Ohrid's healthcare facilities are limited compared to Skopje's.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Cost of Living in Ohrid

In Ohrid, you can rent a one-bedroom apartment for approximately $250 per month. Groceries and dining out are affordable, with a meal at a local restaurant costing about $5. Utilities, including electricity, heating, cooling, water, and garbage, average around $100 per month. The local green market, located near the city center, offers fresh produce at competitive prices.

The cost of living in Ohrid is generally lower than in Skopje, making it an attractive option for retirees on a budget. However, the downside is that Ohrid's smaller size means fewer options for shopping and entertainment. While the city has several supermarkets and small shops, you might miss the variety available in a larger city.

## Lifestyle and Amenities in Skopje

Skopje offers an active lifestyle with numerous cultural events, theaters, and museums. The city is home to the Skopje City Mall, where you can find international brands and a variety of dining options. Public transportation is efficient, with buses connecting different parts of the city. A monthly public transport pass costs around $30.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1680507578133-3db72cf10b25?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8TWFjZWRvbmlhfGVufDB8MHx8fDE3ODQxMDQ3OTN8MA&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Skopje's healthcare facilities are more comprehensive than those in Ohrid. The city hosts several hospitals, including the Acibadem Sistina Hospital, known for its modern facilities and English-speaking staff. However, the activity level of Skopje might not appeal to those seeking a quieter retirement. Traffic congestion and air pollution can be issues, particularly in the city center.

## Housing Options in Ohrid and Skopje

Ohrid offers a range of housing options, from lakeside apartments to houses in the hills. Many properties have impressive views of Lake Ohrid. However, the real estate market can be competitive, especially during the summer months when tourists flock to the area. Engaging a local real estate agent is crucial to effectively handle the market.

In Skopje, you have a wider variety of neighborhoods to choose from. The city center is popular for its proximity to amenities, but quieter areas like Vodno offer a more suburban feel. Rent in Skopje is generally higher than in Ohrid, with a one-bedroom apartment in the city center costing around $400 per month. Be prepared for higher living costs and ensure your budget accounts for this.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

Healthcare in Ohrid is limited, with the main facility being the General Hospital Ohrid. For more specialized care, residents often travel to Skopje. In Skopje, private insurance is recommended to access the best care, especially at private hospitals like Acibadem Sistina.

Visa requirements for U.S. retirees are straightforward, but verify the latest residency rules with official sources. North Macedonia has a flat tax rate of 10%, which is relatively favorable, but consult a tax professional to understand your U.S. tax obligations.

Safety in Ohrid and Skopje is generally good, but as in any city, be aware of your surroundings. Skopje's city center can experience minor issues with pickpocketing, so take standard precautions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Ohrid suitable for retirees who need regular medical care?

Ohrid has limited healthcare facilities, so if you require regular medical attention, Skopje might be a better option due to its comprehensive healthcare services.

### How does the cost of living in Ohrid compare to Skopje?

Ohrid generally has a lower cost of living, with cheaper rent and daily expenses. However, Skopje offers more amenities and healthcare options, which can justify the higher costs.

### What is the best way to find housing in Ohrid and Skopje?

Engage a local real estate agent familiar with the area. They can help you handle the market and find a property that meets your needs and budget.
