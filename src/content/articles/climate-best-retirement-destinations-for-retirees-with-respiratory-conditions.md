# Climate - Best Retirement Destinations for Retirees with Respiratory Conditions — A Local's Perspective

Santa Fe, New Mexico, sits at an elevation of 7,199 feet, offering a dry climate that can be beneficial for retirees with respiratory conditions. The city's low humidity and clean air are often cited as reasons why individuals with asthma or COPD find relief here. However, the high altitude may pose challenges for those not accustomed to thinner air. This article will help you decide if Santa Fe or similar destinations could be a good fit for your respiratory health needs.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want a dry climate and clean air for respiratory health.
- **Monthly reality:** Expect around $3,000 for comfortable living, including housing and healthcare.
- **Biggest advantage:** Low humidity and pollution levels.
- **Biggest warning:** High altitude may require adjustment for some.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Santa Fe, New Mexico: A Breath of Fresh Air

Santa Fe's elevation of 7,199 feet provides a dry climate that can be beneficial for individuals with respiratory issues. The city is known for its low humidity levels, which can help reduce the frequency of asthma attacks or COPD exacerbations. St. Vincent Regional Medical Center is a key healthcare facility offering specialized care for respiratory conditions. While the air quality is generally good, occasional dust storms can be a concern, especially in the spring.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1564740603199-5f56138c6679?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwxMXx8U3BhaW58ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc4NTU3MzYxNXww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

The cost of living in Santa Fe is higher than the national average, with housing being a significant factor. A one-bedroom apartment in the city center can cost around $1,200 per month. The city offers a variety of activities, from art galleries to hiking trails, which can be both relaxing and beneficial for maintaining a healthy lifestyle. However, the high altitude can be a challenge for those not accustomed to it, potentially leading to shortness of breath initially. This makes it crucial for potential residents to spend some time in the area before committing to a permanent move.

## Flagstaff, Arizona: High Altitude and Low Humidity

Flagstaff, Arizona, located at an elevation of 6,910 feet, offers a similar dry climate with the added benefit of cooler temperatures compared to other parts of Arizona. The city is surrounded by the Coconino National Forest, providing plenty of opportunities for outdoor activities, which can be an excellent way to maintain physical health. Flagstaff Medical Center is well-equipped to handle respiratory issues, offering comprehensive care for conditions like asthma and COPD.

![photo](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522778034537-20a2486be803?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5NDQ1NTl8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyMXx8U3BhaW58ZW58MHwwfHx8MTc4NTU3MzYxNXww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)

Housing in Flagstaff is relatively affordable, with average rents for a one-bedroom apartment around $1,000 per month. The city is walkable, and public transportation options are available, though a car may be necessary for more remote areas. One downside is that the high altitude can be challenging for those with severe respiratory conditions, and the pollen season in spring can exacerbate allergies. Consult with a healthcare professional to ensure that the environment will be conducive to your health.

## Asheville, North Carolina: Moderate Climate with Green Spaces

Asheville, North Carolina, offers a moderate climate with a lower elevation of 2,134 feet, making it an attractive option for those who might struggle with higher altitudes. The city is known for its lush green surroundings and numerous parks, which provide clean air and opportunities for outdoor activities. Mission Hospital is a major healthcare provider in the area, offering specialized care for respiratory conditions.

The cost of living in Asheville is relatively moderate, with average rents for a one-bedroom apartment around $1,100 per month. The city's active arts scene and numerous festivals provide ample opportunities for social engagement, which is beneficial for well-being. However, the humidity levels can rise during the summer months, which may not be ideal for everyone with respiratory issues. Visit during different seasons to assess how the climate affects your health.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

When considering a move for respiratory health, verify healthcare access in your chosen destination. Ensure that local hospitals and clinics can provide the specialized care you may need. Private insurance might be necessary to cover specific treatments or medications not available through public healthcare systems.

Visa or residency rules can vary depending on the location, especially if you're considering an international move. Consult with immigration experts to understand the requirements fully.

Taxes can also impact your financial planning. Be sure to understand both U.S. and local tax obligations to avoid unexpected liabilities. Finally, safety and walkability are important considerations. Ensure that the area is safe and that you have access to necessary amenities and emergency services.

## Common Mistakes Retirees Should Avoid

- Failing to verify healthcare facilities and their capabilities.
- Ignoring seasonal climate variations that might affect respiratory conditions.
- Overlooking local transportation options and their accessibility.
- Not considering the cost of living adjustments for housing and daily expenses.
- Skipping a trial visit to assess personal comfort in the new environment.
- Neglecting to check local air quality and pollution levels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does high altitude affect respiratory conditions?

High altitude can lead to shortness of breath and increased heart rate due to thinner air. Consult with a healthcare provider to determine if you can adapt to such conditions comfortably.

### Are there specific healthcare facilities for respiratory conditions in these areas?

Yes, cities like Santa Fe and Flagstaff have hospitals equipped to handle respiratory issues. Verify the availability of specialized care before moving.

### What should I consider regarding transportation in these cities?

While cities like Santa Fe and Asheville offer walkable areas and public transport, having a car may be necessary for more remote locations. Evaluate your transportation needs based on your lifestyle.
