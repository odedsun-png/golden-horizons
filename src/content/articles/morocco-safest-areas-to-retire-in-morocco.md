# Morocco - Safest Areas to Retire in Morocco — A Local's Perspective

In 2022, Morocco recorded a crime rate of just 4.1 per 1,000 inhabitants, making it one of the safer countries in North Africa. Despite common perceptions, Morocco offers retirees a lifestyle that balances affordability with cultural richness. This article will explore some of the safest areas in Morocco where you can comfortably enjoy your retirement.

## What $2,000/Month Actually Buys in Marrakech

With a monthly budget of $2,000, Marrakech offers a surprisingly comfortable lifestyle. In the popular Gueliz district, a two-bedroom apartment can be rented for around $800 per month. This area is known for its modern amenities and proximity to the active local markets. You can easily get your groceries from the Marché Central de Gueliz, where fresh produce and local delicacies are abundant.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/244293/pexels-photo-244293.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

Dining out is another area where your budget will stretch far. A meal for two at a mid-range restaurant typically costs around $30. For those interested in local cuisine, the food stalls at Jemaa el-Fnaa square provide a variety of traditional dishes at just a few dollars each. Public transport is efficient and affordable, with monthly passes for buses and trams costing only about $20.

However, one limitation is that not all locals speak English fluently. While French and Arabic are widely spoken, a basic understanding of either language can be incredibly helpful. Despite this, Marrakech is popular among expats, and you'll find many English speakers within that community.

## Healthcare Facilities in Rabat

Rabat, the capital of Morocco, stands out for its excellent healthcare facilities, which are crucial for retirees. The Clinique Nations Unies and Sheikh Zayed Hospital are two of the top healthcare institutions in the city. Known for their high standards, both hospitals offer services that range from routine check-ups to specialized treatments. Private healthcare insurance is recommended and costs around $100 per month for comprehensive coverage.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/13997789/pexels-photo-13997789.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&dpr=2&h=650&w=940)

In terms of pharmacies, they are widely available and stocked with both local and international medications. Pharmacies like Pharmacie de l’Océan are staffed by professionals who can assist in French and sometimes English. Emergency services are reliable, with ambulances reaching most areas within 10 to 15 minutes.

The city is also home to numerous wellness centers and spas, which can be a boon for maintaining a healthy lifestyle. While Rabat offers excellent healthcare, one potential downside is its higher cost of living compared to other Moroccan cities. However, the trade-off is access to top-notch medical facilities and a generally safer environment.

## Essaouira’s Relaxed Pace and Safety

Essaouira, a coastal town with a crime rate of just 2.5 per 1,000 people, offers a slower pace of life that many retirees find appealing. Known for its historic medina and laid-back atmosphere, Essaouira provides a peaceful yet culturally rich environment. The Medina of Essaouira is a UNESCO World Heritage site, offering narrow streets filled with artisanal shops and cafes.

Housing is affordable, with a typical one-bedroom apartment in the medina costing around $400 per month. The local souks, such as Souk Jdid, provide fresh seafood and produce daily, making it easy to eat healthily on a budget.

Transportation is simple, primarily revolving around walking and local buses, which cost less than a dollar per trip. The town also has a small but friendly expat community, making it easier to integrate and find new friends.

Essaouira is generally quieter than larger cities, which might be challenging for those who prefer a more active environment. However, its safety makes it an ideal place for those looking to enjoy their retirement years in peace.
