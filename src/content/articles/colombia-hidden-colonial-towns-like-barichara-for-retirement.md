# Colombia - Hidden Colonial Towns Like Barichara for Retirement — A Local's Perspective

Barichara, a small colonial town in Colombia, is home to just over 7,000 residents and is known for its cobblestone streets and well-preserved Spanish architecture. Located in the Santander department, Barichara offers a slower pace of life compared to Colombia's busy cities. This article will help you decide if Barichara or similar hidden colonial towns in Colombia are the right fit for your retirement plans.

## Quick Take

- **Best for:** American retirees who want a peaceful, historical setting with a strong sense of community.
- **Monthly reality:** Expect to spend around $1,500 to $2,000 on living expenses, including rent, food, and healthcare.
- **Biggest advantage:** Lower cost of living compared to urban areas and the opportunity to immerse in local culture.
- **Biggest warning:** Limited access to specialized healthcare services.
- **Before moving:** Verify visa, healthcare, tax, housing, and insurance rules with official sources or a qualified professional.

## Cost of Living in Barichara

Renting a modest two-bedroom house in Barichara can cost as little as $400 per month, significantly lower than in larger Colombian cities like Bogotá or Medellín. Utilities, including water, electricity, and internet, typically add another $100 per month. The local market on Calle 5 offers fresh produce at a fraction of the cost you might expect in the U.S., with a week's worth of groceries often totaling less than $50.

Dining out is affordable, with a meal at a local restaurant costing around $5. However, while the cost of living is low, Barichara's remoteness can make accessing certain goods and services more challenging. Imported goods, for instance, can be expensive and limited in availability.

## Healthcare Access and Considerations

The local healthcare center, Centro de Salud de Barichara, provides basic medical services, but for more specialized care, residents often travel to San Gil, about a 30-minute drive away. San Gil offers more comprehensive healthcare facilities, including the Clínica Santa Cruz de la Loma. Private health insurance is crucial to ensure access to a broader range of services, especially if you require ongoing medical treatment.

While healthcare costs in Colombia are generally lower than in the U.S., the quality and availability of services can vary. Retirees should carefully evaluate their healthcare needs and consider proximity to medical facilities when choosing a retirement location in Colombia.

## Lifestyle and Community in Barichara

Life in Barichara is centered around community and tradition. The town's main square, Parque Principal, is a center of activity where locals gather for cultural events and festivals. Retirees often find themselves participating in local activities, such as art classes or volunteering, which can help in building a social network.

While the town offers a peaceful lifestyle, it might not suit those who thrive on the pace of city life. Limited entertainment options and the slower pace can be a downside for some. However, for those seeking tranquility and a close-knit community, Barichara provides an ideal setting.

## Healthcare, Visa, Tax, and Safety Notes

Before deciding to retire in Barichara, ensure you understand the healthcare system. While basic services are available locally, more serious medical needs may require travel. Private health insurance is advisable to cover any gaps.

Regarding visas, Colombia offers a retirement visa for those who can demonstrate a monthly income of at least three times the Colombian minimum wage. This requirement is subject to change, so consult official sources or a qualified professional.

Tax obligations for Americans retiring in Colombia can be complex. Understand both U.S. and Colombian tax laws to avoid unexpected liabilities. Safety in Barichara is generally good, but as with any location, it's wise to visit and assess the area personally.
