## Start With Housing—Then Compare the Rest

Can $3,500 a month support two retirees? Start with the biggest bill: the place you’ll call home.

California’s rental costs vary dramatically. Here is how three cities compare for a one-bedroom apartment:

| California city | Monthly one-bedroom rent | Money remaining for all other expenses |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fresno | $1,200 | $2,300 |
| San Diego | $2,322 | $1,178 |
| San Jose | $2,500 | $1,000 |

The last column is not spending money. It still has to cover groceries, utilities, transportation, healthcare, entertainment, and unexpected bills.

In Fresno, that leaves a couple considerably more room to work with. In San Diego or San Jose, rent alone takes roughly two-thirds or more of the entire budget.

Sources: Zillow’s rental-market pages for [Fresno](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/fresno-ca/), [San Diego](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/san-diego-ca/), and [San Jose](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/san-jose-ca/), reviewed November 24, 2026. Figures are market averages for one-bedroom apartments, not quotes for specific homes.

### What About Portugal?

Portugal deserves the same test—not a different set of numbers.

Comparing California rent with an estimate of Portugal’s entire monthly budget would be misleading. So would comparing dollars with euros without accounting for the exchange rate.

Instead, start with a Portuguese apartment you could actually rent year-round. Convert that rent into dollars using the exchange rate and fees you would pay. Then subtract it from the same $3,500 budget.

Here is an example showing how that calculation works. These are hypothetical rent amounts, not claims about current Portuguese rental prices:

| Monthly rent, expressed in U.S. dollars | Money remaining from $3,500 |
|---|---:|
| $1,000 | $2,500 |
| $1,300 | $2,200 |
| $1,600 | $1,900 |
| $2,000 | $1,500 |

Now the comparison is fair: rent against rent, with everything expressed in dollars.

If you find a suitable Portuguese apartment for the equivalent of $1,300 a month, you would have $2,200 left for the rest of your expenses. That is more breathing room than the San Diego example—but slightly less than the Fresno example.

Housing is only round one. Next, price healthcare, groceries, utilities, transportation, taxes, and trips back to the United States for your household.

Portugal wins financially only if the complete budget works—not because a café lunch costs less.

### The Takeaway

Don’t ask whether Portugal is cheaper than California. Ask whether the Portuguese neighborhood you would choose offers a better life on your budget than the California community you would otherwise call home.

A paid-off house in California and a rented apartment in Lisbon are two very different starting points. So are a San Diego rental and a modest home in a smaller Portuguese town.

Compare the lives you could actually live. Leave the postcard out of the spreadsheet.
