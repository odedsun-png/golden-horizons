# Ukraine - Comparing Borscht in Kyiv and Lviv

## Quick Taste

Borscht, a sour soup common in Eastern Europe, prominently features beetroots and varies significantly between regions and cities.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/36010447/pexels-photo-36010447.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&fit=crop&h=627&w=1200)

## The Story Behind the Dish

Borscht has longstanding roots in Ukrainian history, symbolizing comfort and tradition in Ukrainian households.

## How Locals Eat It

Typically enjoyed as a first course, borscht is accompanied by a dollop of sour cream and a side of rye bread.

## Where to Find It

Kyiv and Lviv offer distinct takes on borscht, with each city's restaurants serving variations reflective of their culinary influences.

![photo](https://images.pexels.com/photos/7816667/pexels-photo-7816667.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&fit=crop&h=627&w=1200)

## City vs. Countryside Flavor

While urban borscht may feature more refined ingredients, rural variants often lean on simplicity and locally-sourced produce.

## Cost Portions and Ordering Tips

Portions are typically generous, and prices remain affordable throughout Ukraine. Combining with traditional sides is recommended for a full experience.

## What to Know Before You Try It

Familiarize yourself with the variety of ingredients as they can change the overall taste and texture of the dish.

## Common Mistakes Visitors Make

Assuming all borscht tastes the same can lead to disappointment; it's crucial to explore the different versions offered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the main ingredients in traditional Ukrainian borscht?

The main ingredients typically include beetroot, cabbage, potatoes, onions, carrots, and sometimes meat such as beef or pork.

### Is borscht served hot or cold?

Borscht can be served either hot or cold, with the warm version more common in Ukrainian cuisine.

### How different is Kyiv's borscht from Lviv's borscht?

Kyiv's borscht often incorporates more European influences, while Lviv's version might have a richer concentration of local flavors.

### Can vegetarians enjoy borscht in Ukraine?

Yes, many restaurants offer vegetarian versions of borscht, omitting meat while focusing on the rich beet flavor.
