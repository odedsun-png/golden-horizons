## What a Da Nang Banh Mi Is

The baguette came to Vietnam with the French in the 1860s. French forces landed in Da Nang in 1858. For decades the sandwiches stayed close to French habits, such as ham and butter or liver pâté.

A distinctly Vietnamese style took shape in Saigon in the 1950s. The fillings you will see today include liver pâté, cold cuts, shredded pickled carrot and daikon, fresh cilantro, cucumber, mayonnaise and chili sauce.

We found no reliable source on what is specific to Da Nang, so we do not claim a local filling or a famous stall. Ask the person at the counter what they are known for.

## How to Order and What to Ask

Point at the filling you want and ask the price before the stall starts building the sandwich. If you do not want chili or cilantro, say so up front.

Pick a stall with a steady line of locals and food that is cooked and served hot. Skip anything that has sat out lukewarm.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Da Nang safe for Americans?

Vietnam is Level 1, "Exercise Normal Precautions," in an advisory dated June 23, 2026, and it warns that petty crime is common in popular tourist locations. ([cadataapi.state.gov](https://cadataapi.state.gov/api/TravelAdvisories))

### When did the baguette come to Vietnam?

The French colonial administration introduced the baguette in the mid-19th century, and sources date it to the 1860s. ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A1nh_m%C3%AC))

### Does Medicare cover me if street food makes me ill?

Usually no. Original Medicare generally does not cover care outside the U.S., and the cruise-ship exception applies only within 6 hours of a U.S. port. ([medicare.gov](https://medicare.gov/coverage/travel-outside-the-u.s.))

### How do I lower the risk of eating street food?

The CDC says germs that cause food poisoning grow fastest between 40°F and 140°F, so choose food served steaming hot, and use hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol. ([wwwnc.cdc.gov](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/food-water-safety))

### How did the sandwich change after the French left?

Vietnam gained independence in 1954, and the sandwich was redesigned around then with pork belly, egg, pickles and herbs. ([tastingtable.com](https://www.tastingtable.com/1874795/french-roots-vietnamese-banh-mi-sandwich))
