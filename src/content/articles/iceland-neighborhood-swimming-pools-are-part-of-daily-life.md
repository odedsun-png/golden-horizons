# Iceland's Geothermal Pools Anchor Daily Life

> **Did you know?** Geothermal energy heats nearly every swimming pool in Iceland, turning the country’s vast hot water supply into a cornerstone of daily life and social culture.

In Iceland, geothermal energy is not just a natural resource; it's a way of life. Nearly every swimming pool across the country is heated by this abundant energy source. This unique feature makes swimming pools a warm and inviting place for locals and visitors alike.

In every town around Iceland, you will find these warm and refreshing swimming pools. They are more than just places to swim; they are integral to the social fabric of Icelandic communities. People gather here to relax, socialize, and enjoy the soothing warmth of the geothermal waters.

The culture surrounding these pools is a testament to how Icelanders have embraced their natural resources, turning them into a vital part of their daily lives and social interactions.
