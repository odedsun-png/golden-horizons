Public care and private care are different choices. Mexico’s Instituto Mexicano del Seguro Social, known as IMSS, is the public agency to ask about enrollment routes and eligibility. Medicare is not a substitute for a Mexico City care plan. It generally does not cover routine care outside the United States, so ask each insurer and hospital how treatment, deposits, and reimbursement work before you move.

### A Simple Public-Care Enrollment Check

Start at an IMSS office near the address where you would live. Bring your passport, Mexican residency document, proof of your local address, and any identification or forms IMSS asks for. Ask for the current eligibility route, the enrollment wait time, and a written list of costs before you rely on public coverage.

Then compare that answer with private quotes from AXA Seguros and GNP Seguros. Ask both insurers for the same 12-month plan design. That gives you a fairer view than comparing one broad quote with another policy that leaves out outpatient care or chronic conditions.
