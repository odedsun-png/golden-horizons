## What a $350/mo Two-Bedroom in Tbilisi Really Looks Like: Rent, Furnishings, and Neighborhoods

Renting a two-bedroom apartment in Tbilisi for $350/month is possible, particularly in neighborhoods outside the city center. In districts like Saburtalo or Didube, older or less centrally located apartments often fall within this price range. Furnished units typically command higher rents, with prices ranging from $400 to $700 in more central areas like Vake or Vera.

Utilities, including electricity, heating, water, and internet, generally add $100 to $150 to monthly expenses. Public transport is affordable, with bus and metro fares costing less than $0.50 per ride, making it easy to navigate the city without a car.

## Residency Pathways for Retirees in Georgia: How to Get a Long-Term Stay (Visa Types, Documents, Costs)

Georgia offers several residency pathways for retirees, primarily through income or asset-based permits. The Residente Permit requires proof of income or substantial savings. The State Services Development Agency outlines the necessary documents, including proof of income, a valid passport, and application forms. Processing times vary, but permits are generally issued within a few months.

Healthcare in Georgia for foreigners relies heavily on private insurance. Public healthcare is limited for non-citizens, so enrolling in a private plan is advisable. Costs for private insurance vary, with monthly premiums depending on age and coverage level.
