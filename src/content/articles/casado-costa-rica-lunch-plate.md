# Casado: Costa Rica's Everyday Lunch Plate and a $12 San José Price Check

**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING — VERIFY COSTS

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** The casado is Costa Rica's traditional lunch: rice, beans, plantains, salad and usually 1 protein on one plate. Numbeo's San José average for a meal at an inexpensive restaurant is $12.14, with a reported range of $8.94 to $20.11. The catch is that this number covers all cheap meals, not casados alone, and we found no current casado-only price we could verify. Ask the price before you sit down, and test 1 casado at a soda before you plan a week of lunches around it.

## What Is on a Casado Plate

The core is rice, black beans, plantains and a salad, with an optional protein such as chicken, beef, pork or fish. A tortilla or a hard-boiled egg often comes with it, and picadillo, a chopped vegetable hash, can stand in for the protein or the vegetables.

Unlike gallo pinto, the rice and beans on a casado are served separately, not mixed. Gallo pinto is the breakfast version of those same two ingredients.

## Why It Is Called "Married"

Casado is Spanish for "married." Two explanations are common. One says customers at restaurants asked for the same plate a married man ate at home. The other says the rice and beans are "married" because they always appear together. Neither is settled, so treat both as local stories.

## Where and When to Eat It

Lunch is the main meal of the day in Costa Rica, and the casado is the traditional lunch. Sodas began in cities in the 1960s to feed workers and schoolchildren, and the casado is especially common at them. Some places may serve it in the evening too, so ask.

## Practical Comparison

### What a casado costs against other San José meals

**What the evidence says:** Numbeo lists an inexpensive-restaurant meal in San José at $12.14, a mid-range meal for 2 at $67.04, and a cappuccino at $4.62, all checked November 24, 2026. A soda casado may cost less than the $12.14 average, but we could not verify a current soda price.

**What to check next:** Ask for the full price including tax and service before you order, and compare it with 1 other soda on the same street.

### Casado against gallo pinto

**What the evidence says:** The casado is the lunch plate, with rice and beans served apart. Gallo pinto is the breakfast dish with the two mixed together.

**What to check next:** Try both in the same week at the same soda and note which plate fills you for longer.

### Casado against a mid-range restaurant

**What the evidence says:** The $67.04 mid-range figure for 2 people works out to $33.52 each, three courses without drinks. Numbeo's cheap-meal average is well under that, so a set lunch plate fits a daily budget better than a sit-down meal.

**What to check next:** Write down what you pay for 5 lunches and compare the total with your monthly food budget.

## The Trade-Off

**What you may gain:** A filling, familiar meal built around rice, beans and a protein, in an informal setting where locals eat.

**What you may give up:** A single clear price. Menu prices at sodas vary, and the only city-wide number we could verify is a general cheap-meal average. If you are watching a fixed income, ask the price first every time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is in a casado?

A casado has 5 usual parts: white rice, black beans, plantains, a protein, and a salad or cooked vegetables, often with a tortilla or hard-boiled egg ([theinternationalkitchen.com](https://theinternationalkitchen.com/blog/costa-ricas-favorite-meal-the-casado))

### How much does a casado cost in Costa Rica?

We could not verify a casado-only price, but a meal at an inexpensive restaurant in San José averages $12.14, with a range of $8.94 to $20.11, based on Numbeo's November 24, 2026 listing ([numbeo.com](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/San-Jose-Costa-Rica?displayCurrency=USD))

### Why is it called casado?

Casado means "married," and there are 2 common explanations: the plate a married man ate at home, or rice and beans that are always paired ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casado))

### Is the casado only a lunch dish?

It is the traditional lunch, and it appears on many lunchtime menus with a choice of 3 proteins: fish, chicken or a pork chop, so ask whether a place serves it later in the day ([afar.com](https://www.afar.com/magazine/the-best-costa-rican-food))

### Is Costa Rica safe for older visitors?

The U.S. State Department rates Costa Rica Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution, due to crime, in an advisory dated April 1, 2026, and notes that petty crime is common ([cadataapi.state.gov](https://cadataapi.state.gov/api/TravelAdvisories))
