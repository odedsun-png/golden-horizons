# Finance - Could Your Social Security Check Buy You a Completely Different Retirement Abroad?

**Golden Horizons Status:** 🟡 WORTH TESTING

> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** Many retirees can continue receiving Social Security benefits abroad, but the purchasing power varies by destination. SSA payment continuity depends on country-specific rules, and healthcare and residency require separate planning. Testing destinations with a 30-day trial can help determine if your Social Security check stretches further abroad.

## Retirement Snapshot

**Planning-budget lens:** SSA payments can continue abroad, but eligibility depends on country-specific rules. Use the SSA Payments Abroad Screening Tool for verification.  
**Primary decision:** Determine which destinations allow a Social Security-supported lifestyle within your benefit amount by testing with current cost benchmarks.  
**Residency:** Residency rules are country-specific and separate from SSA payment eligibility. Consult official immigration sources.  
**Healthcare:** Foreign healthcare access depends on the host country. Budget separately from SSA discussions.  
**Housing / daily life:** Use 1-bedroom rent and typical monthly expenses as a starting frame in each destination.  
**Safety:** Safety requires local, destination-specific data. National advisories do not translate into town-level safety rankings.  
**Best fit:** A destination where housing, groceries, utilities, and transport align with your SSA benefit and lifestyle needs.  
**Biggest warning:** Do not conflate SSA payment rules with residency/visa eligibility or foreign healthcare access.

## The Planning-Budget Reality

SSA payments can continue outside the U.S. in many destinations, but country-specific rules apply. Do not assume universal continuity or denial. Cost evidence by destination is essential to determine whether a fixed SS income stretches or not; destination-specific rent, groceries, utilities, and transport data are needed for concrete budgeting.

## The Residency Reality

Residency rules are destination-specific and must be sourced from official immigration authorities for each country. SSA does not determine foreign residency eligibility.

## Practical Comparison

- **SSA payment continuity:** Use the SSA Payments Abroad Screening Tool to verify if benefits continue outside the U.S.  
- **Purchasing power context:** Compare 1-bedroom rent, groceries, utilities, and transport for at least two destinations.  
- **Residency vs. SSA rules:** Navigate visa/residency independently of SSA payment continuity.  
- **Healthcare access:** Plan for local healthcare costs and access, separate from U.S. Medicare coverage.

## The Golden Horizons 30-Day Test

1. Select two destinations for initial testing based on preliminary cost and SSA-continuity signals.
2. Arrange short-term housing to approximate 1-month rent and test lease norms.
3. Grocery-shop and cook typical meals for a month to gauge food costs.
4. Test local public transportation and typical commute needs.
5. Visit nearby healthcare facilities to understand access and proximity.
6. Use the SSA Payments Abroad Screening Tool to confirm payment-continuity status for chosen destinations and verify any documentation needs.
