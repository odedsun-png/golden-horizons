## The Residency Reality

Neither Hungary nor the Czech Republic offers a retirement-specific visa for U.S. retirees. Instead, retirees must qualify for general long-term residence permits, which require proof of sufficient financial means, accommodation, and health insurance. While both countries allow for long-term stays, the lack of a dedicated retirement visa means retirees must navigate these general requirements.
