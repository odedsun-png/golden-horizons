But travel access and permission to live in Bulgaria are different questions. The available evidence does not establish a new 2026 rule giving American retirees a special right to settle there. For a longer stay, the practical divide remains clear: visiting is one matter; obtaining Bulgarian long-stay permission or residency is another.

### Healthcare May Cost Less Per Visit — But Coverage Is the Bigger Question

A brief private doctor appointment is around $44, a useful benchmark for a routine visit. It is not a complete healthcare budget. Specialist consultations, prescriptions, imaging, emergency treatment, and hospital care need their own answers before you commit to a long stay.

For private care in Sofia, Acibadem City Clinic, Tokuda Hospital, and Lozenetz Hospital are institutions to contact directly about appointments, English-language support, and payment arrangements.
