# Spend Christmas Day at the Beach in Australia

> **Did you know?** Australians spend Christmas Day at the beach.

In Australia, Christmas is celebrated with a unique twist. Instead of snow, you'll find sparkling white sand underfoot. It's a time when locals head to the beach, donning their red cossies and Santa hats, ready to enjoy the sun and sea.

A typical Aussie Christmas lunch might feature fresh oysters, prawns, and lamb cutlets sizzling on the barbecue. This festive meal is often enjoyed right on the beach, adding to the relaxed and joyful atmosphere.

Activities like swimming, surfing, and sunbathing are popular ways to spend the day. The beach becomes a vibrant hub of holiday cheer, with families and friends gathering to celebrate in the warm summer weather.

So, if you're in Australia during the holiday season, be prepared to swap your snow boots for flip-flops and join in the beachside festivities!
