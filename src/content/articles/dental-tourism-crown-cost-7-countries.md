## Seven-Country Dental Crown Cost Snapshot
Dental tourism offers significant savings on dental crowns, but costs vary by country. In Thailand, crowns range from $300 to $500 (~$300-550), depending on the clinic and materials. Mexico offers crowns for $400 to $700 (~$400-770), often including consultation fees. In Hungary, known for its dental tourism, expect to pay $250 to $450 (~$250-495). Costa Rica's prices are similar, ranging from $300 to $600 (~$300-660). In Colombia, crowns cost $350 to $550 (~$350-605), while in India, prices are as low as $250 to $400 (~$250-440). Finally, in the Philippines, crowns range from $300 to $500 (~$300-550).

## Cost Context by Category and Typical Clinic Tier
The cost of a dental crown depends on several factors, including the clinic's tier and the materials used. High-end clinics in urban areas may charge more, but they often provide English-speaking staff and modern facilities. For example, a ceramic crown in a top-tier clinic in Thailand might cost $500 (~$550), while a similar crown in a mid-tier clinic in Mexico could be $400 (~$440). Consultation fees typically range from $50 to $100 (~$55-110), and follow-up visits may be extra unless specified.

## Residency Pathways for Dental Tourism Stays
For extended dental care trips, short-term medical visas are available in most countries. In Thailand, a medical visa requires proof of income around $1,000/month and allows stays of up to 90 days. Mexico offers a similar visa with a processing time of about 10 days. In Hungary, a Schengen visa permits stays up to 90 days, but requires comprehensive travel insurance. Each country has specific document requirements, so check the official immigration websites for details.
