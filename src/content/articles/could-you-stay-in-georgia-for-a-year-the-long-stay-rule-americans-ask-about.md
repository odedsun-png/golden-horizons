## Georgia Stay Mechanics for Americans (visa-free and long-term options)
Imagine strolling through the charming streets of Tbilisi, where the scent of freshly baked khachapuri fills the air. This vibrant city, with its blend of history and modernity, offers Americans the possibility of a visa-free stay for up to one year. Under current regulations, U.S. citizens can enjoy a 365-day stay without needing a visa, making Georgia an appealing option for retirees looking for a temporary home abroad.

For those considering a longer commitment, Georgia provides long-term visas with 90-day or 12-month validity. These visas allow multiple entries, offering flexibility for those who wish to explore neighboring countries. However, it's crucial to verify the latest requirements and processing times through the Georgian Migration Service and your nearest consulate, as regulations can change.

## Cost of Living Scenario: Solo Retiree in Tbilisi (2026)
Living in Tbilisi offers a cost-effective lifestyle compared to many Western cities. A one-bedroom apartment in the city can range from $350 to $900 per month, depending on the district. Areas like Vake and Mtatsminda tend to be pricier, while more affordable options can be found in Old Tbilisi.

Groceries and dining are also budget-friendly. A typical grocery basket, including fresh produce and essentials, costs around $175 to $250 monthly. Dining out is a treat, with a modest dinner at a local restaurant costing about $10. Public transportation is efficient and affordable, with monthly passes available for under $20.

Utilities, including internet, add approximately $100 to $150 to your monthly expenses. Overall, a solo retiree can expect to spend between $900 and $1,200 per month, excluding private health insurance.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Can Americans stay for a full year in Georgia without a visa?
Yes, Americans can stay in Georgia visa-free for up to one year under certain rules. Verify the current ordinance for your passport category. (Source: [matsne.gov.ge](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/2882028?utm_source=openai))

### What is the typical monthly rent for a 1-bedroom in central Tbilisi in 2026?
The rent commonly ranges from $550 to $900+ in central areas, while outside the center, it ranges from $350 to $700. (Source: [unlim.ge](https://unlim.ge/en/articles/housing-for-rent-in-tbilisi?utm_source=openai))

### What is a realistic solo monthly budget in Tbilisi (rents, groceries, utilities, transport, dining) in 2026?
A realistic budget is roughly $900 to $1,200 excluding private health insurance, with rent being a major cost driver. (Source: [relocation.ge](https://relocation.ge/en/cost-of-living-in-georgia/?utm_source=openai))

### What documents are typically required for a Georgian long-term residence visa?
Official migration rules list required documents and steps; consult the Migration Service page for the exact list and current fees. (Source: [sda.gov.ge](https://sda.gov.ge/en/products/migration-residence-permits/?utm_source=openai))

### How does Georgia healthcare for foreigners typically work?
Private facilities are common; public care exists but may require local eligibility and insurance. Compare private hospitals and costs. (Source: [sda.gov.ge](https://sda.gov.ge/en/products/migration-residence-permits/?utm_source=openai))
