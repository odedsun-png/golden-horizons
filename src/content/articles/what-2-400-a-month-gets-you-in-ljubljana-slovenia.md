A basic core budget comes to $1,457-$2,016, leaving $384-$943 before healthcare, travel, and larger surprises. A cappuccino runs about $2.10, a casual lunch about $9-$14, and dinner for two about $38. Housing choice is the part that decides whether this feels roomy or careful.

## Where the Budget Starts: Your Apartment

Ljubljana is not the bargain-basement version of Europe. Expatistan ranked it as the second-most-expensive capital in Eastern Europe in September 2026, which makes rent the first number worth studying.

A furnished studio in a normal Ljubljana area is listed around $753 monthly. In a more expensive area near central Ljubljana, that same small-home category reaches about $1,038. That difference may not sound dramatic at first, but it determines how much room remains for healthcare, travel, and the ordinary pleasures that make a move feel sustainable.

Šiška is one practical area to inspect during a scouting stay, especially if you are willing to trade some central proximity for more room in the budget. Central Ljubljana is the expensive comparison point; these citywide studio figures are useful planning anchors, but the exact building, heating setup, and furnishing level still matter.

A larger furnished home changes the calculation quickly. An 85-square-meter apartment is about $1,180 in a normal area and about $1,760 in an expensive one—before the power and heating bills arrive.

### Food That Feels Like Daily Life, Not Deprivation

The grocery math is gentle in small moments. A liter of whole milk is about $1.71, a dozen large eggs about $3.82, and a loaf of bread about $1.33.

A market bag can hold apples at about $2.04 per kilogram, potatoes at about $1.36 per kilogram, and tomatoes at about $2.98 per kilogram. Those prices are not a complete monthly food bill, but they support a food-only planning allowance of $250-$350 for one person who cooks most meals.

Ljubljana’s central markets give those figures a human shape: fruit, bread, and vegetables rather than a spreadsheet of abstract categories. The food budget gets less forgiving when imported products, special diets, and frequent restaurant meals become routine.

### The Bills That Arrive After You Move In

For a one-person, 45-square-meter apartment, utilities work out to roughly $235 monthly, with internet adding about $28. A larger two-person setup can bring the combined household bill closer to $337.

This is the question to ask before signing anything: is heating included, and what happened to the bill during the coldest winter month? A bright apartment can look like a bargain at $753 until an unclear heating arrangement turns it into something else.

## Residency, Healthcare, and the Questions to Settle First

A monthly budget and the legal right to stay are two different questions. Slovenia has long-stay and residency pathways that require documented income or savings, standard documents, and a route suited to your circumstances.

That means $2,400 may support everyday life without automatically proving residency eligibility. Before you fall in love with a particular Šiška rental, ask the Slovenian consulate which documents apply to your intended stay and whether your insurance arrangement meets the current rule.

For care, foreign residents generally use Slovenia’s public-health route or private coverage according to residence status and insurance. University Medical Centre Ljubljana and Zdravstveni dom Ljubljana are useful names to learn during a scouting month, while ZZZS is the public health insurance institution you will hear about in any serious discussion of coverage.

Private care gives one concrete budget marker: a private doctor visit is about $59. Medicare does not normally pay for care outside the United States, so keep your U.S. Medicare choices separate from whatever Slovenian public or private coverage you arrange.

The small daily pleasures remain modest: a $3.50 beer at a neighborhood pub and a $12 taxi are possible without making every Tuesday a splurge. But healthcare and residency are the two questions to settle before calling the city affordable for your own situation.
