## The Residency Reality

The D7 visa offers a direct route for retirees to reside in Madeira. To qualify, applicants must demonstrate sufficient passive income, have health insurance, and meet documentation requirements. The initial visa is typically valid for four months, allowing entry into Portugal, with a residence permit issued after arrival, valid for one year and renewable. Permanent residence is possible after five years of continuous legal residence.

Retirees should apply for the D7 visa through the Portuguese consulate in their home country and be prepared to provide proof of income, health insurance, and accommodation in Portugal. It's essential to verify the latest requirements and ensure all documentation is in order before applying.
