## Cost of a Two-Bedroom Tbilisi Apartment and Monthly Living Budget for a Solo American Retiree in 2026

Imagine waking up in Tbilisi, Georgia, where the streets blend old-world charm with modern vibrancy. Renting a two-bedroom apartment with a balcony here can range from $600 to $1,200 per month, largely influenced by the district. Central areas like Vake and Old Town are on the higher end, while neighborhoods like Saburtalo offer more budget-friendly options.

But housing is just the beginning. A realistic monthly budget for a solo retiree, excluding healthcare, falls between $650 and $1,000. This budget covers groceries, utilities, internet, local transport, and dining out. For example, a loaf of bread costs around $0.50, and a casual meal at a local restaurant might set you back $5 to $10. Public transport remains a bargain, with a monthly pass priced at about $15.

**Estimated core monthly spend:** $650-$1,000  
**Remaining buffer vs the $2,000 budget:** +$1,000

**How your money compares to the US:** That $5 lunch in Tbilisi would cost around $15 in the US. A $15 monthly transport pass in Tbilisi compares to $100 in many US cities.

**Income requirement:** Varies by visa type — confirm with the consulate

**A Typical Day in Tbilisi**  
Start your day with a $1.50 coffee at a local café. For lunch, enjoy a $5 meal at a neighborhood restaurant. Spend the afternoon exploring the city using a $0.30 bus ride. End your day with a $10 dinner at a cozy eatery.

## Residency Options and Healthcare Access for Foreigners in Georgia

Georgia doesn't offer a specific retirement visa, but there are alternative pathways for residency. Many expats opt for standard residence permits. It's essential to check the latest Georgian government portals or consular guidance for the most accurate information.

Healthcare in Georgia is a mix of public and private options. While private insurance is commonly used by expats, it's crucial to contact local hospitals and insurers for quotes. Outpatient visit costs can vary, so obtaining accurate information is vital.
