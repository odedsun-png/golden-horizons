# A Two-Bedroom Tbilisi Apartment With a Balcony: What Would You Actually Pay?
> **THE SHORT ANSWER:** Renting a two-bedroom apartment with a balcony in Tbilisi costs between $600 and $1,000+ per month, depending on the neighborhood and condition. A comfortable monthly budget for a solo retiree, excluding healthcare, ranges from $1,500 to $2,000. Residency options are available, but healthcare requires private insurance or out-of-pocket payments.

## What It Would Cost Now: The 2-Bedroom Tbilisi Apartment with Balcony
Renting a two-bedroom apartment in Tbilisi varies significantly based on location and amenities. In central and expat-friendly districts like Vake, rents tend to be on the higher end, ranging from $800 to $1,400 per month. In contrast, outer districts offer more affordable options, with rents starting around $600. Utilities and internet add approximately $100 to $140 monthly, while groceries and basic food can cost between $250 and $350. Local transportation is affordable, with monthly costs around $40 to $60.

**Estimated core monthly spend:** $1,240-$1,800  
**Remaining buffer vs the $2,000 budget:** +$200 to -$760

**How your money compares to the US:** That $4 lunch? Your usual Panera combo back home is $12. A $40/month private plan covers what a $450 US premium buys.

**Income requirement:** Varies by visa type — confirm with the consulate

**A Typical Day in Tbilisi**  
Start your day with a coffee at a local café for about $2.30. Enjoy a casual lunch at a nearby eatery for $4.60 to $6.20. A bus ride across town costs just $0.30 to $0.60.
