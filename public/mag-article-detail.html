Healthcare Quality in Panama for Expats — What Retirees Need to Know

By the Golden Horizons Editorial Team Reviewed by Golden Horizons Editors Updated: April 26, 2026  ·  3 min read
Panama City’s private hospital district — where many expats receive care rivaling U.S. standards at a fraction of the cost.

When considering retirement in Panama, one of the key factors is healthcare quality. With a budget of $2,000 to $5,000 a month, many find Panama’s healthcare system both affordable and surprisingly comprehensive.

◆
From the Health File
Panama City’s private hospitals are popular with expats because many doctors train internationally, appointments are faster than in the U.S., and routine care can cost far less than comparable U.S. visits.
Why Retire Here

Panama offers a mix of modern amenities and a lower cost of living, making it a popular destination for retirees. Cities like Panama City and David boast state-of-the-art hospitals and clinics, often with English-speaking staff. Many doctors have trained in the U.S. or Europe — a reassurance for those concerned about maintaining their health standards.

The country’s tropical climate and vibrant culture are additional draws. Retirees often appreciate the slower pace of life, coupled with activities ranging from beach outings to cultural festivals. The warmth of the local community helps smooth the transition, though patience and a willingness to adapt are essential.

— ✦ —
Practical Tips
Many expats choose to live near Panama City to stay close to top-tier private medical facilities.

Understanding the healthcare system is crucial before relocating. Panama has both public and private options, but expats generally prefer private facilities for shorter wait times and higher perceived quality. Research hospitals and clinics in your area of interest before settling down.

◆
From the Visa File
Panama’s Pensionado visa gives retirees discounts on everything from flights to restaurant bills.

International health insurance is popular among expats, offering coverage in Panama and abroad. Compare different plans and providers carefully — premiums and coverage vary significantly. Language can be a barrier, but learning basic Spanish healthcare phrases goes a long way. Local expat communities are invaluable for recommendations.

— ✦ —
Cost & Healthcare
Routine specialist visits in Panama City run $50–$100. Hospital stays average $100–$200/day.

Healthcare costs in Panama are significantly lower than in the U.S. Routine doctor visits cost as little as $20 to $60. Specialist consultations range from $50 to $100. Hospital stays run approximately $100–$200 per day — a fraction of U.S. rates.

Monthly insurance premiums range from $100 to $300 depending on coverage and health factors. Review each plan carefully, including restrictions and waiting periods for pre-existing conditions. Access to care is generally excellent in urban areas, though rural residents may need to travel to larger cities for specialist care.
